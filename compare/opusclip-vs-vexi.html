OpusClip calls itself an AI video editor and clipping tool: paste a video link or drop a raw take, and it clips, edits, sizes and publishes the clips to social platforms [1]. It picks the moments in a long video, reframes them, adds captions and B-roll, and writes titles, descriptions and hashtags [1][2][3]. Its scheduler posts to YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Pages, LinkedIn Business and X [3]. On its pricing page today, Starter is $15 a month, billed monthly, and Pro is $29 a month billed monthly or $174 billed annually [2]. It also has a $0 plan with 60 credits a month and a watermark, whose clips can no longer be exported after 3 days [2].

VEXI is an AI content studio that runs social media for entrepreneurs and creators. You describe what you want and it makes the posts, videos, designs and captions, then schedules and publishes them. Plans start at $199 a month, and every plan starts with a 15-day trial.

The difference is where it starts. OpusClip starts from a video you already have, a link or a raw take [1]. A feed also needs posts made from a photo, an idea or nothing at all, designed in your brand, every week. VEXI starts from anything and keeps posting. VEXI costs more than OpusClip: plans are $199, $399 or $599 a month [2]. What you pay for is the work you no longer do.

VEXI makes it for you: you describe what you want and it makes the posts, videos, designs and captions; you approve everything, or let it run on its own, and it schedules and publishes them.

OpusClip vs VEXI, asked and answered.

Does VEXI do what OpusClip does?

For clipping, yes: send VEXI a long recording like a podcast and you get its best clips back, captioned. VEXI also makes posts from a photo, a link or an idea, designs them in your brand, and posts them.

Does OpusClip post to social media?

Yes: OpusClip's pricing page lists auto posting to YouTube Shorts, TikTok and IG Reels on Starter, and Pro adds a social media scheduler [2]. Its scheduler page lists auto-posting to YouTube, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, TikTok, Facebook Page, LinkedIn Business and X [3].

How much does OpusClip cost?

On OpusClip's pricing page today, Starter is $15 a month, billed monthly only, Pro is $29 a month billed monthly or $174 billed annually, and Business has custom pricing [2].
