KCM (Keeping Current Matters) is a content membership for real estate agents. Starting at $59.95/month, you get access to pre-written blog posts, social graphics, and national market data branded with your logo. Much of KCM's shareable content is the same for every member — their own FAQ says so. The platform auto-posts to Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn, but does not list TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest or Google Business Profile. Video is ready-made weekly clips plus the RealTalk teleprompter tool, included on both plans.

VEXI (formerly Social Realtr) is an AI content studio that runs social media for entrepreneurs and creators. You describe what you want and it makes the posts, videos, designs and captions, then schedules and publishes them. Plans start at $199 a month, and every plan starts with a 15-day trial.

The core question: do you want generic national content that every other agent shares, or original content designed for your brand and your market? Here is how they compare.

KCM vs VEXI, asked and answered.

Does KCM create custom local content for my market?

Partially. KCM provides national market data and blog posts that are the same for all members. Their KCM Local feature adds zip-code stats and a Graphic Builder for local charts, but blog posts, videos and most graphics are shared templates branded with your details. VEXI makes graphics and captions in your brand.

Does KCM include professional video editing?

KCM’s Elite plan ($99.95/month) includes RealTalk, a teleprompter and video recording tool with pre-written scripts. But there is no professional video editing — you record yourself reading a script and the video is auto-formatted. VEXI includes professional short-form video editing on every plan.

Does KCM post to all social media platforms?

KCM offers automated posting to Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. KCM does not list native posting to TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest or Google Business Profile.

Is KCM content unique to me or shared with other agents?

KCM’s own FAQ states that some shareable content is the same for all members. Blog posts, social graphics, and video scripts are standardized templates branded with your logo and colors — but agents in overlapping markets may share identical content. VEXI makes graphics in each client’s own brand.
