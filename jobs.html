How hiring goes.

Four steps, honest feedback at each one, usually done inside three weeks.

  1. 01

    A conversation

    Thirty minutes with someone who does the work. What you have built, what you want to build next.

  2. 02

    Something real

    A small piece of actual work, paid, on your own time. No trick questions and no take-home marathons.

  3. 03

    The team

    You walk us through what you made and we argue about it properly. This is the best part.

  4. 04

    Offer

    Same week. If it is a no you will hear why.
