What VEXI is

VEXI is software you subscribe to — social media, handled. You tell it what you want, and it makes the posts, videos, designs and captions, then schedules and publishes them to the social media accounts you connect.

03

Your free trial

  • Every plan — Creator, Pro, Max — starts with a 15-day trial.
  • We take your card when the trial starts. Nothing is charged during the trial.
  • Everything is unlocked during the trial. The only limit is your plan's monthly usage.
  • At the end of day 15, the trial becomes your paid plan and we charge the price you chose at checkout — monthly, or yearly if you picked yearly. Then we charge the same price every month (or every year) until you cancel.

  • One trial per customer, ever — not one per plan.

Buying at one of our live events? There's no trial. Your plan starts, and is charged, when you buy.

04

Plans, billing and renewals

  • Plans are billed monthly or yearly, in advance, in US dollars: Creator $199 · Pro $399 · Max $599 a month, or Creator $1,592 · Pro $3,192 · Max $4,792 a year.
  • Yearly billing gives you four months free on every plan: you pay for eight months and get twelve. If you were already on a yearly plan before September 30, 2026, you stay on the price you bought until you change it.
  • If you cancel a yearly plan, it runs to the end of the year you paid for and then stops. Months you did not use are not refunded.
  • The price you see at checkout is the price you pay. Any tax we must charge is shown there too, before you pay.
  • Your plan renews automatically on the same date every month — or every year on yearly billing — until you cancel.
  • By starting a plan, you authorize us to charge your card for it every month — or every year, if you choose yearly billing — until you cancel.
  • Each plan includes a monthly amount of usage for making things. It resets every month; unused usage doesn't carry over.
  • Usage you buy on top of your plan never expires, and it's used only after your monthly usage is gone. Unused monthly usage isn't refunded.
  • Each plan includes connected social media accounts: Creator 2 · Pro 4 · Max 8+.
  • If you buy extra usage, the app shows the price before you confirm.
  • If a payment fails, everything keeps working for 7 days while you update your card, and we remind you. If it still hasn't gone through after 7 days, we cancel your plan and email you.
  • We may change our prices. We'll email you at least 30 days before a new price applies to you, and you can cancel before it does.

If you choose yearly billing, you pay for the year up front and your plan renews every year.

05

Cancelling

Cancel any time in the app, from your billing settings. There's no notice period and no exit interview.

Your plan keeps running until the end of the time you've paid for, then stops, and we don't charge you again. If you cancel during your trial, you're never charged.

Posts already published to your social media accounts stay there — they're on your accounts, not ours.

To delete your account, cancel your plan first, then ask us at support@vexi.app and we delete it once your plan has stopped.

06

After your plan ends

Your files stay in your account for 90 days after your plan ends, and you can download them any time. Export my files on your billing page puts everything in one download; its link works for 7 days.

We email you 7 days before the 90 days are up. After that, your files leave your account and move to our long-term storage.

Your account, your brand profile and your records stay, so you can pick up where you left off if you come back.

We keep the records listed under Records we keep for billing questions for at least 24 months after your account closes.

07

Refunds

We don't refund time or usage you've already paid for — not part of a month, not usage you didn't use, not extra usage or add-ons you bought, and not a month your account sat unused.

Cancelling stops your next charge. Your plan runs to the end of the time you've paid for.

If we charged you by mistake — twice for the same thing, the wrong amount, or after you cancelled — tell us and we refund that charge in full.

Not happy with something VEXI made? Edit it or have it made again. That isn't a billing mistake.

Think a charge is wrong? Email support@vexi.app first — it's the fastest way to fix it.

When you cancel, we may email you a few of the pieces we made for you, so you can keep them.

Posting on your behalf

We post to your connected social networks on your behalf. You approve the first piece; after that it runs on its own.

Until you approve a piece yourself — or schedule a post of your own — nothing our AI writes for you is published. After that first approval, new pieces are published on their own, on the schedule the app shows you.

Want to see every post first? Turn on approvals in your account settings and every piece waits for your yes again. You can switch it back any time.

You let us use your likeness — your face, your voice, and the photos and footage you upload — to make your content, including editing it with AI.

We use your likeness only to make content for you. We never use it for anyone else, and we never use it to train an AI model.

How VEXI uses AI

VEXI uses AI — ours and our providers' — to write, design, edit and check content. AI can get things wrong, so check names, prices, addresses and claims before anything goes out.

Everything VEXI makes can be edited. You choose whether posts wait for your approval or publish on their own, and you can change that any time.

VEXI doesn't give legal, financial or other professional advice. Make sure your posts follow the rules of your industry and where you work — including advertising, housing, lending and health rules.

We may refuse or remove content that breaks these rules. Every upload to the demo is checked automatically before our AI sees it, and the five kinds above are refused there with one plain line. The AI providers we use run their own safety filters too, and may refuse a request.
