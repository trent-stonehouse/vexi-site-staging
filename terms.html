Your free trial

  • Every plan — Creator, Pro, Max — starts with a 15-day trial.

Plans, billing and renewals

  • Plans are billed monthly, in advance, in US dollars: Creator $199 · Pro $399 · Max $599 a month.

Cancel any time by emailing support@vexi.app from the address on your account. We confirm by email. There's no notice period and no exit interview.
