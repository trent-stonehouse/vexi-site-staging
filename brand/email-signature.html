Install it

A Google Workspace admin console cannot set a different signature for each person without a third-party tool or a script, so with a handful of mailboxes each person pastes once. Whoever answers from a team mailbox pastes the team version into that account.

Gmail on the web

  1. Open Gmail. Click the gear, then See all settings.
  2. On the General tab, scroll to Signature and click Create new. Name it.
  3. Click Copy signature above, then paste into the signature box.
  4. Under Signature defaults, set it for new emails and for replies and forwards.
  5. Scroll to the bottom and click Save changes.

The Gmail app on an iPhone

The app keeps its own mobile signature, and it is text only.

  1. Click Copy plain text above.
  2. In the app, open the menu, then Settings, then your address.
  3. Tap Signature settings, turn Mobile signature on, and paste.

Apple Mail on a Mac

  1. In Mail, open Settings (Preferences on older systems), then Signatures.
  2. Pick the account on the left, click the plus button and name the signature.
  3. Click Copy signature above and paste into the box on the right.
  4. Untick "Always match my default message font".
  5. In Choose Signature at the bottom, pick it so every new message carries it.

Outlook

  1. Open Settings, then Accounts, then Signatures (on the web and in the new Outlook; classic Outlook for Windows: File, Options, Mail, Signatures).
  2. Click New signature and name it.
  3. Click Copy signature above and paste into the editor.
  4. Set it for new messages and for replies and forwards, then Save.
