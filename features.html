FEATURES

Everything it does.

You describe what you want and it makes the posts, videos, designs and captions, then schedules and publishes them. The same studio on your phone and on your desk. Pick a view to see how each part looks where you work.

01 / JUST ASK

Type what you want. It comes back finished.

  • Posts, videos, designs and captions, made in the conversation.
  • Cut, with B-roll and captions, without opening an editor.
  • Say what you wanted instead and it remakes it.
02 / AI AUTOPILOT

It writes it, designs it and puts it out.

  • Posts at the times your audience actually shows up.
  • Approve everything, or let it run on its own.
  • Set how designed the posts should look and it holds that mix.
See how it works: AI AUTOPILOT
03 / DESIGN EDITOR

Tap anything on the design to change it.

  • Headline, photo, logo, colors, QR code.
  • Your brand files and voice applied every time.
  • No layers, no canvas, no export settings.
04 / IMAGE TO VIDEO

One still becomes a moving clip.

  • A photo turns into motion, sized for the feed.
  • Pick a look and it re-cuts to match.
  • Comes back ready to post.
05 / VIDEO EDITING

One long video. The best clips back.

  • Upload a long raw recording, like a podcast.
  • Get back the clips that work best, captioned.
  • Music is laid under the cut, matched to every clip.
06 / LIKE THIS VIDEO

Show it a video you like.

  • Drop a clip, or paste a link — if a platform blocks the link, drop the file instead.
  • VEXI studies the pacing, captions and energy, then makes yours the same way.
  • Your words, your footage, never theirs.
07 / SCRIPT + TELEPROMPTER

Read. Record. Ship.

  • Tell it what the script should do and it writes it.
  • Your script rolls on screen while you record.
  • Three-second countdown before it starts.
08 / TRENDING IDEAS

Know what people are searching.

  • Pulled from YouTube, Google and TikTok.
  • Ranked by how popular each search is.
  • One tap turns an idea into a script or a post.
09 / ANALYTICS

Numbers that tell you what to do next.

  • Plain-English notes on what is working.
  • It finds the times your audience shows up.
  • Every number in one place — performance, content, reports.
