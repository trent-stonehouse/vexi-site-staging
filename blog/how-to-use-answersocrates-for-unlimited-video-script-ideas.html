What Is AnswerSocrates and Why Realtors Need It

AnswerSocrates.com is a free research tool that shows you the top questions people are asking about any topic in any location. It pulls from search engine data to surface the most frequently used queries—giving you a direct window into what your potential clients are wondering about.

AnswerSocrates

A free web tool that aggregates the most common questions people search for on Google about any topic. For realtors, it reveals exactly what buyers and sellers in your specific city or area are asking—giving you an unlimited source of content ideas backed by real search demand.

Unlike brainstorming content ideas from your own head, AnswerSocrates gives you data-backed topics. Every question it surfaces is something real people are actually typing into Google. That means when you create content answering those questions, you’re speaking directly to existing demand—not guessing at what might be interesting.

The location filter is what makes this tool especially powerful for real estate. You can see what people in Nashville are asking versus what people in Miami are asking. Your content becomes hyper-local and hyper-relevant.

How to Use AnswerSocrates: Step-by-Step

  1. Go to AnswerSocrates.com

    Open the website and type a keyword related to your niche. Try “buying a home,” “selling a house,” “mortgage rates,” “first-time homebuyer,” or any topic your ideal clients care about.

  2. Set Your Location

    Change the location filter to your target market. If you serve Nashville, set it to Nashville. This ensures the results reflect what YOUR potential clients are searching for, not a national average.

  3. Hit Search and Browse the Results

    You’ll see dozens of questions organized by category—who, what, when, where, why, how, and more. Each question is a potential video, Reel, carousel, or blog post.

  4. Pick a Question and Copy It

    Choose any question that resonates with your expertise. The more specific the question, the more targeted your content will be.

One search on AnswerSocrates typically returns 50–100+ questions. Run five different keyword searches and you have 250–500 content ideas. That’s over a year of daily content without repeating a single topic.

Turn Questions Into Ready-to-Shoot Video Scripts With AI

Once you have a question from AnswerSocrates, the next step is turning it into a polished video script. Here’s the exact prompt to paste into ChatGPT:

The Script Generator Prompt

“This is a question my ideal client asked about my area of expertise. I’m a realtor who helps [ideal avatar] achieve [dream result] without [what they don’t want]. Take this question and turn it into a short-form script for Instagram using: a short hook, proof of credentials, context around the hook, deliver value by answering the question, and a CTA to follow. Give me three hook variations: one negative hook, one question hook, and one desire hook.”

This prompt gives you three complete scripts from a single question. Three different hooks, three different angles, all answering the same question your ideal client is already searching for. That’s three pieces of content in under five minutes.

The three hook variations are key:

  • Negative hook: “Never do this when buying a home in [city]” or “Stop making this mistake”
  • Question hook: “Is it possible to buy a home with no money down in [city]?”
  • Desire hook: “If you want to get pre-approved fast, follow these steps”

How to Build a Full Month of Content in One Sitting

Here’s how to turn AnswerSocrates into a complete content calendar for the month:

  1. Run 5 Keyword Searches

    Use keywords like “buying a home,” “selling a house,” “mortgage rates,” “first-time buyer,” and “real estate investing.” Each search returns 50–100 questions.

  2. Pick 10 Questions

    Select the 10 most relevant questions across all your searches. Prioritize questions that match your expertise and your ideal client’s biggest concerns.

  3. Generate 30 Scripts

    Run each question through the AI prompt to get 3 scripts per question. That’s 30 scripts—one for every day of the month.

  4. Batch Record

    Set aside one afternoon to record all 30 videos. If each video takes 3 minutes to record, you’re done in 90 minutes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AnswerSocrates free to use?AnswerSocrates offers free searches with some limitations. The free tier gives you enough data to generate dozens of content ideas per session. For most realtors, the free version is more than sufficient to build a full content calendar.
Can I use AnswerSocrates for content besides videos?Absolutely. The questions it surfaces work for Instagram Reels, TikTok videos, YouTube content, carousel posts, blog articles, email newsletters, and even listing presentation talking points. One question can be repurposed across multiple formats.
How is AnswerSocrates different from just Googling questions?AnswerSocrates aggregates and organizes search data into visual maps of questions, prepositions, and comparisons. It shows you the full landscape of what people are asking about a topic, not just the top 10 results. The location filter makes it especially powerful for local real estate content.
How often should I use AnswerSocrates for new content ideas?Once a month is usually enough. A single session can generate 30–50 content ideas. Run your searches at the beginning of each month, generate your scripts, batch record, and you’re set for the entire month.
What keywords should realtors search on AnswerSocrates?Start with broad terms: “buying a home,” “selling a house,” “mortgage,” “real estate agent.” Then go niche: “first-time homebuyer,” “VA loan,” “foreclosure,” “investment property,” “relocation.” Match the keywords to your ideal client avatar.
