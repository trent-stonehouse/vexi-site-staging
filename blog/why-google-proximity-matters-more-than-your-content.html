Why Google Proximity Matters More Than Your Content

You can mention nearby cities in your services section to rank higher in those areas, but you’ll only dominate where your physical address is located. If you’re trying to get clients in a city more than 45 minutes away from your Google Business Profile address, stop. You’re wasting your time. Google proximity in real estate is the #1 ranking factor, and no amount of content, keywords, or reviews can override it.

Understanding google proximity real estate ranking is the difference between dominating your market and being invisible in it.

The Truth About Google Proximity in Real Estate

Google ranks you based on proximity first. Before relevance. Before activity. Before reviews. The physical address on your Google Business Profile determines what city you dominate in local search.

Here’s how google proximity real estate ranking actually works:

  • When someone searches “realtor near me,” Google looks at their location and your GBP address, then calculates the distance between the two
  • The closer your address is to the searcher, the more likely you are to appear in the top results
  • You’ll rank highest in the city where your GBP address is physically located
  • As distance increases, your ranking drops — rapidly. A 10-mile difference can push you from the 3-Pack to page 3

This is why your GBP address matters more than almost anything else in your local SEO strategy. You can have perfect keywords, 100 five-star reviews, and weekly posts — but if your address is 45 minutes from the searcher, you’re not showing up.

Google Proximity

Google proximity refers to the physical distance between the searcher’s location and your business address listed on your Google Business Profile. It is the primary ranking factor for local search results, including the Google 3-Pack and Google Maps.

The Mistake: Trying to Rank in Cities Far Away

The biggest google proximity real estate mistake agents make is trying to rank in a big city far away from their actual address. If your GBP says Franklin, Tennessee, but you’re trying to dominate Nashville searches — you’re muddying the water. You’ll never dominate Nashville without a Nashville address.

Here’s what happens when agents spread too thin:

  • They dilute their local authority — Instead of dominating one city, they rank poorly in multiple cities
  • Their content targets too many markets — A post about “buying in Nashville” doesn’t help you rank in Franklin where your address actually is
  • They waste time on SEO that can’t work — No amount of keyword optimization can override a 45-minute proximity gap
  • They miss the opportunity to dominate their own city — While chasing bigger markets, they lose ground in the market where they could actually win
“I see realtors trying to rank in the big city far away. You’re just muddying the water. You’ll never dominate there without an address there.”
— Trent Stonehouse, CEO of VEXI (formerly Social Realtr)

The Strategy: Focus All Efforts on Your Main City

Focus ALL your efforts on the city where your Google Business Profile address is located. That’s where you’ll rank highest, get the most visibility, and generate the most leads. Own that city completely before even thinking about expanding.

Here’s the right approach to google proximity real estate:

  1. Dominate Your Primary City First

    Make every piece of content, every review, every GBP post about your primary city. Become the undisputed local expert that Google has no choice but to recommend. Your address city is your home base — own it completely.

  2. Mention Nearby Cities in Your Services Section

    You can still rank in nearby areas by adding those cities to your services section. “Realtor in [nearby city]” as a service helps you appear in searches from adjacent markets. You’ll rank higher in nearby areas than distant ones.

  3. Build Reviews That Mention Your City

    Get clients to include your city name in reviews. When Google sees “best realtor in Franklin” in 25 reviews, it reinforces that you’re the most relevant agent for Franklin searches.

  4. Post Weekly to Your Google Business Profile

    Weekly posts with local content and city-specific keywords show Google you’re active and relevant. Proximity + relevance + activity = dominant local ranking.

Want to Rank in Multiple Cities? You Need Multiple Addresses

If you want to dominate more than one city, you need a real address in each city. This is the reality of google proximity real estate ranking. Google doesn’t let you fake your way into a market — you need a physical presence there.

Here are your options for multi-city ranking:

  • Open a satellite office in each city you want to dominate. A co-working space or shared office with a real address gives Google the proximity signal it needs.
  • Partner with agents in other cities who can refer clients to you and vice versa. You focus on dominating your city; they focus on theirs.
  • Accept the limitation and become the undisputed #1 in your primary city. One city dominated is worth more than 5 cities where you’re barely visible.

The agents who try to rank everywhere end up ranking nowhere. The agents who focus on one city and dominate it completely are the ones who build a sustainable, lead-generating machine from Google search.

79% of people click on one of the top 3 Google results (the 3-Pack) when searching for local services. If you’re not in the 3-Pack for your city, you’re losing thousands in potential commission every month.

Source: Backlinko, Google CTR Statistics, 2025

How Proximity, Relevance, and Activity Work Together

Google proximity real estate ranking doesn’t exist in a vacuum. It works alongside two other factors: relevance and activity. Together, these three factors determine your entire local search ranking.

  • Proximity (most important): How close is your GBP address to the person searching? This is the factor you can’t fake. Your address determines your territory.
  • Relevance: How relevant is your profile to what they’re typing? If someone searches “realtor in Nashville” and your profile mentions “realtor in Nashville” multiple times across services, reviews, and posts — Google sees you as highly relevant.
  • Activity: How active is your business on Google? Dead profiles from 2020 don’t get recommended. Weekly posts, fresh reviews, and updated content show Google you’re alive and serving customers.

Proximity gets you in the game. Relevance and activity win the game. You need all three working together, but without proximity, the other two can’t save you. Focus on your address city, load it with keywords, and stay active — that’s the complete formula.
