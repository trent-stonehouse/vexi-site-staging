The New Agent’s 90-Day Social Media Launch Plan (Start From Zero)

TRENT STONEHOUSE11 MIN READAPRIL 1, 2026

⚡ Quick Answer

New real estate agent social media success comes down to a structured 90-day launch. Days 1–7: set up optimized profiles on Instagram and Facebook with a professional headshot, local keywords, and a clear bio. Days 8–30: post 3–4x per week using local content, market tips, and personal brand posts. Days 31–60: add video content, engage daily, and build a content calendar you can sustain. Days 61–90: layer in Google Business Profile, start running local ads, and convert followers into conversations. You don’t need thousands of followers. You need the right 500 people in your ZIP code.

Why 90 Days Changes Everything for a New Real Estate Agent on Social Media

Ninety days is the minimum effective dose for social media traction. Not because the algorithm needs exactly 90 days to notice you — but because you need 90 days to build the habits, content library, and local visibility that make social media a lead generation channel instead of a time sink.

“Marketing is compound interest.”

That quote isn’t motivational fluff. It’s math. A post you publish in Week 1 gets indexed by Google. It gets shared to a few people. One of them follows you. They see your Week 4 post and send it to a friend who’s thinking about selling. That friend calls you in Week 9. Every piece of content you publish adds to the pile, and the pile starts working for you 24/7.

The agents who quit after 3 weeks never reach the inflection point. The agents who push through 90 days almost never stop — because by then, the results are undeniable.

Days 1–7: Setup (Build Your Digital Storefront)

Before you post anything, your profiles need to look like they belong to a professional. A potential client will visit your Instagram or Facebook page, scan it for 3 seconds, and decide if you’re worth following. Get this right first.

Platform Selection

Start with two platforms only: Instagram and Facebook. Instagram is where real estate content gets discovered. Facebook is where homebuyers and sellers over 30 spend their time. Do not spread yourself across six platforms. Two done well will outperform six done poorly every time.

Profile Optimization Checklist

Instagram:

  • Switch to a Business or Creator account (unlocks analytics)
  • Professional headshot — not a selfie, not a group photo
  • Name field: “Your Name | [City] Real Estate Agent” (this is searchable)
  • Bio: who you help + where + a call to action. Example: “Helping first-time buyers in Phoenix find their home. DM me your questions ↓”
  • Link in bio: your website, Linktree, or booking page
  • Highlight covers: About Me, Listings, Local Tips, Reviews

Facebook:

  • Create a Business Page (separate from your personal profile)
  • Same headshot and branding as Instagram
  • Complete every field in the “About” section — city, service area, specialties
  • Pin a welcome post or intro video to the top
  • Join 5–10 local community Facebook Groups in your market

Day 1–7 Action Items

  • ✓ Set up and optimize both profiles
  • ✓ Create 3–4 Instagram Highlight categories with placeholder covers
  • ✓ Write your first 8 post captions (batch them now, schedule them later)
  • ✓ Follow 50 local accounts: businesses, community pages, other agents, lenders
  • ✓ Post your first piece of content by Day 5

Days 8–30: Foundation (Build the Posting Habit)

This is where most new agents fail. The profile looks good, the first few posts go up, and then … silence. The foundation phase is about building the habit that carries you through the rest of the plan.

Posting Frequency

Post 3–4 times per week on each platform. This is the minimum frequency that keeps you visible in local feeds. If you can do more, great. But 3 per week every week beats 7 per week for two weeks followed by nothing.

Content Mix for Weeks 2–4

Use the 3-pillar content rotation:

  1. Local content (40%) — Neighborhood spotlights, local business features, community events, “best of” lists for your area
  2. Educational content (40%) — Homebuyer tips, market stats, mortgage rate updates, “what to expect” guides
  3. Personal brand content (20%) — Behind-the-scenes of your day, your story of becoming an agent, your “why,” community involvement

Posts with local content receive 3.5x more engagement than generic real estate posts. Tagging local businesses and locations in your posts increases reach by an average of 79% because the algorithm shows your content to people who follow or interact with those locations.

Source: Sprout Social Industry Benchmarks, 2025

Daily Engagement (15 Minutes)

Posting without engaging is like dropping flyers from a helicopter. You need to be in the comments, the DMs, and the local groups. Spend 15 minutes daily:

  • 5 minutes: Comment on 10 local posts (not “nice!” — real comments that add value)
  • 5 minutes: Reply to every comment and DM on your own posts
  • 5 minutes: Engage in 1–2 local Facebook groups (answer questions, share helpful info — no self-promotion)

Week 2–4 Action Items

  • ✓ Post 3–4x per week on Instagram and Facebook
  • ✓ Engage for 15 minutes daily using the 5-5-5 method
  • ✓ Film and post your first short-form video (even if it’s a 30-second market update)
  • ✓ Create a simple content calendar (Google Sheets works)
  • ✓ Follow 20 more local accounts per week

Days 31–60: Consistency (Lock In Your Rhythm)

By Day 30, you should have 12–16 posts live on each platform. You have a small but growing audience. Now the goal shifts from building a habit to building a system — one you can maintain for the next 12 months without burning out.

“Content is King. Consistency is Queen.”

— Trent Stonehouse, CEO of VEXI

Content Batching

Stop creating content day-by-day. Block 2 hours every Sunday to create and schedule the entire week’s content. Here’s how:

  • 30 min: Write 4 captions
  • 30 min: Design 4 graphics (Canva has free real estate templates)
  • 30 min: Film 1–2 short videos on your phone
  • 30 min: Schedule everything using a tool like VEXI or Later

Add Video

If you haven’t started posting video yet, this is the phase where it becomes non-negotiable. Instagram Reels get 2x the reach of static posts. You don’t need fancy equipment. Your phone, natural lighting, and a quiet room are enough.

Start with these easy video formats:

  • “One thing every first-time buyer should know” (30 seconds)
  • “3 things I love about [Neighborhood]” (45 seconds)
  • Weekly market stat update (15–30 seconds)
  • Day-in-the-life clips (Stories format)

Google Business Profile

During this phase, set up your Google Business Profile if you haven’t already. This is a separate channel from social media but critical for new real estate agent social media strategy. Post weekly updates to your GBP, add your service areas, upload photos, and start collecting character reviews from people who know your work ethic.

Days 31–60 Action Items

  • ✓ Switch to weekly content batching (2-hour Sunday sessions)
  • ✓ Post at least 1 Reel or short-form video per week
  • ✓ Set up and optimize your Google Business Profile
  • ✓ Ask 5 people for character reviews on Google
  • ✓ Review your analytics: which posts got the most saves and shares?
  • ✓ Double down on what’s working, cut what’s not

Short-form video content generates 1,200% more shares than text and image content combined. For new real estate agents, Reels and Shorts are the fastest path to organic reach because the algorithm distributes video to non-followers at a much higher rate than static posts.

Source: Social Insider Benchmarks Report, 2025

Days 61–90: Growth (Convert Followers to Clients)

You have 60 days of content live. Your profiles have authority. Your engagement is building. Now it’s time to turn visibility into conversations and conversations into clients.

“Visibility + Consistency + Value = TRUST”

— Trent Stonehouse, CEO of VEXI

The DM Strategy

Every new follower gets a DM within 24 hours. Not a sales pitch. A genuine welcome. Example: “Hey [name]! Thanks for the follow. Are you local to [City]? Always great to connect with people in the area.”

This one habit alone generates more leads than any post ever will. The goal is to start a conversation, not close a deal. People do business with people they’ve talked to.

Story Engagement Loops

Use Instagram Stories to create two-way interaction:

  • Polls: “Would you rather have a big backyard or a short commute?”
  • Question boxes: “What’s your biggest question about buying a home?”
  • Quizzes: “Can you guess the median home price in [City]?”

Every response is a DM. Every DM is a conversation. Every conversation is a potential client.

Start Running Local Ads (Optional but Powerful)

With $5–10/day, you can boost your best-performing posts to reach more people in your ZIP code. Target by location (your city + 10-mile radius), age (25–55), and interests (real estate, home buying, home improvement). Boost posts that already performed well organically — the algorithm has already validated them.

Add LinkedIn

By Day 60, consider adding LinkedIn as your third platform. LinkedIn is the referral network. Connect with lenders, title reps, home inspectors, insurance agents, and other professionals who send referrals. Share your market knowledge posts there. The audience is smaller but the lead quality is higher.

Days 61–90 Action Items

  • ✓ DM every new follower within 24 hours
  • ✓ Post 3–5 interactive Stories per week
  • ✓ Test $5/day boosted posts on your top-performing content
  • ✓ Set up LinkedIn and post 2x per week
  • ✓ Track your first 90-day metrics: followers, engagement rate, DMs, and leads generated
  • ✓ Review your content calendar and plan for the next 90 days

What to Post When You Have Zero Listings

The biggest objection new agents have: “I don’t have anything to post.” Wrong. You have an entire city full of content. Listings are maybe 10% of effective real estate social media. Here are the content types that build authority without a single sold sign:

Local Business Spotlights — Visit a local coffee shop, restaurant, or boutique. Take a photo, tag them, and write why you love the spot. The business reshares it. Their audience sees you. Free exposure to hundreds of local followers.

Neighborhood Guides — “5 Things You Didn’t Know About [Neighborhood].” Include walkability scores, nearby schools, average home prices, and hidden gems. This is the content that ranks on Google and positions you as the local expert.

Market Updates — Pull data from your MLS or Redfin. “Median home price in [City] this month: $425,000 (up 3.2% from last year).” Simple graphic. Huge credibility.

First-Time Buyer Tips — “3 things your lender won’t tell you.” “The hidden cost of buying a home.” “What credit score do you actually need?” Educational content builds trust faster than any listing photo.

Behind-the-Scenes — Show what your actual day looks like. Previewing homes, writing offers, attending training. Authenticity beats polish on social media every time.

5 Mistakes That Kill a New Agent’s Social Media Momentum

  1. Posting inconsistently. Three posts in one day, then nothing for two weeks. The algorithm penalizes gaps. Pick a frequency you can sustain and stick to it.
  2. Only posting listings. If you don’t have listings, you have nothing to post. If you only post listings, nobody cares. Mix in local content, education, and personality.
  3. Ignoring engagement. Posting and ghosting. Social media is a two-way conversation. If you’re not responding to comments and DMs within a few hours, you’re leaving leads on the table.
  4. Trying to be on every platform. Master two platforms first. Adding TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, and X simultaneously is a recipe for burnout and mediocre content everywhere.
  5. Waiting for perfection. Your first 20 posts won’t be great. Post them anyway. The agent who posts imperfect content consistently will always beat the agent waiting for the perfect photo, caption, and hashtag set.

What matters is that your followers are people in your target market — not random accounts. A new real estate agent with 400 engaged local followers will outperform an agent with 10,000 bot followers. Focus on your ZIP code, not vanity metrics.
What social media platforms should a new real estate agent start with?Instagram and Facebook. Instagram is the top platform for real estate content discovery. Facebook is where most homebuyers and sellers over 30 spend their time. Once you have a consistent posting rhythm on those two, add Google Business Profile for local SEO and LinkedIn for referral networking. Two platforms done well beats six done poorly.
How often should a new real estate agent post on social media?3–4 posts per week minimum. Consistency matters more than volume. Posting 3 times per week every week for 90 days builds more trust and reach than posting daily for two weeks and then going silent. Use a content calendar and batch your content on one day per week to make it sustainable.
What kind of content should a new agent post with no listings or sales?Local business spotlights, neighborhood guides, market stats, homebuyer tips, and behind-the-scenes content. You don’t need listings to build authority. Agents who position themselves as local experts through community content often generate leads faster than agents who only post sold signs.

A new agent who shows up consistently with local, relevant content will build visibility faster than a 20-year veteran who posts once a month.
