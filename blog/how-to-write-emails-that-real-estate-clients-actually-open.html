How to Write Emails That Real Estate Clients Actually Open

TRENT STONEHOUSE4 MIN READAPRIL 1, 2026

⚡ Quick Answer

To boost email open rates in real estate, write like a human, not a marketer. Use 1–2 sentence paragraphs, generous line breaks, conversational tone, and always include a P.S. Send local content — events, restaurant reviews, weekend guides — instead of boring market stats. Local event emails get 35–50% open rates versus under 15% for traditional real estate newsletters.

You’re sending emails. Your clients aren’t opening them. Sound familiar? The problem isn’t email as a channel — it’s what you’re putting in those emails. Most agents write like they’re drafting a corporate brochure when they should be writing like they’re texting a friend.

Improving email open rates in real estate starts with one realization: your email list is owned attention. Unlike social media where the algorithm decides who sees your content, every person on your list chose to hear from you. That’s powerful. Don’t waste it with boring newsletters nobody asked for.

“Email isn’t dead. Yours just sucks. Until now.”

The DotComSecrets Writing Formula for Real Estate Emails

Trent Stonehouse teaches all his agents the DotComSecrets writing formula — and it’s the single biggest lever for improving email open rates in real estate. Here’s the framework:

  1. 1–2 Sentences Per Paragraph

    Short paragraphs feel scannable. Long blocks of text feel like homework. Your reader is on their phone — respect that.

  2. Use Line Breaks Generously

    White space makes everything feel lighter. More line breaks = more reading = higher engagement.

  3. Talk Like You’re Texting a Friend

    “Hey [First Name], just a heads up — [City] is throwing a huge night market this Saturday downtown.” That’s it. That’s the format. Casual, warm, direct.

  4. Always Include a P.S.

    People scan. They skim. They jump straight to the P.S. Make it personal: “P.S. I’m trying to hit every ice cream spot in [City] this month. Let me know your favorite.”

Subject Lines That Actually Get Opened

Your subject line is the gatekeeper. If it sounds like marketing, it gets deleted. If it sounds like a friend, it gets opened. The best subject lines for email open rates in real estate are short, local, and curiosity-driven:

  • “This weekend in [City] 🎉” — Event-driven, local, casual
  • “You need to try this spot” — Sounds like a text from a friend
  • “3 homes under $500K right now” — Specific and valuable
  • “Quick question for you” — Curiosity + personal
  • “I almost made a huge mistake this week” — Story-driven, vulnerable

Notice what’s missing? No “Monthly Market Update — March 2026.” No “Your Real Estate Digest.” No corporate-sounding headers. Write subject lines that feel like they came from someone who knows you.

Emails with localized subject lines see 26% higher open rates than generic subject lines. Adding your city name makes the email feel personal and relevant.

Source: Campaign Monitor, Email Benchmarks, 2024

The Email Template That Outperforms Every Market Report

Here’s what a high-performing real estate email actually looks like. This format consistently gets replies and boosts email open rates in real estate:

Winning Email Template

Hey [First Name],

Just a heads up — [City] is throwing a huge night market this Saturday downtown.

It’s packed with food trucks, live music, and apparently the best fried chicken in the city (I’ll report back).

Here’s the link if you want to check it out: [Link]

Also — if you know anyone looking to buy or sell this summer, I’ve got some great opportunities coming down the pipeline. Just hit reply and I’ll fill you in.

Talk soon,
— [Your Name]

P.S. I’m trying to hit every ice cream spot in [City] this month. Let me know your favorite.

That email will outperform any 600-word market report. It’s personal, local, easy to read, and has a natural call-to-action buried in the middle — plus a P.S. that invites a reply.

You’re Not Writing to Sell — You’re Writing to Remind

The biggest mindset shift for email open rates in real estate: you’re not writing to close. You’re writing to stay present. Every opened email is a touchpoint. Every reply is a conversation. Every month you show up builds familiarity.

When their friend or cousin mentions real estate, you’re the name they remember. Not because you pitched them — but because you showed up consistently in their world with content that actually mattered to them.

“The agents who show up monthly earn the referrals the agents who don’t will never hear about.”

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good email open rate for real estate agents?Traditional real estate newsletters average under 15% open rates. But local event-focused emails achieve 35–50% open rates. The difference is content quality — people open emails that feel personal and locally relevant, not corporate market reports.
How do I improve my email open rates in real estate?Use the DotComSecrets formula: short paragraphs, generous line breaks, conversational tone, and always include a P.S. Write subject lines that sound like a text from a friend, not a marketing department. Send local content instead of market stats.
Should real estate agents include a P.S. in their emails?Always. People scan emails and jump straight to the P.S. Make it personal, fun, or curiosity-driven. Something like: “P.S. I’m trying to hit every ice cream spot in [City] this month. Let me know your favorite.” It invites replies and builds connection.
What email topics get the highest open rates for realtors?Local event roundups, restaurant recommendations, weekend activity guides, personal stories, and quick market snapshots. Anything that connects to their city, community, and family. Avoid generic inventory stats and mortgage rate updates.
