Blackmagic Camera App Settings for Professional Real Estate Video

The Blackmagic Camera app settings for professional real estate video are: 24 fps (cinematic look), 1/48 shutter speed (double your frame rate), lowest ISO possible (light the room instead), locked white balance (no color shifting), manual focus (set it once), and Flat or Film profile (more room to color-grade). Frame Rate and Shutter Speed: The Cinematic Foundation

Set your frame rate to 24 frames per second. This is the frame rate used in movies and high-end video production. It creates that smooth, cinematic motion blur that makes your videos feel professional rather than “phone footage.”

Set your shutter speed to 1/48 — or double your frame rate. This is called the 180-degree shutter rule, and it’s the single most important setting for cinematic video. When your shutter speed is double your frame rate (24 fps x 2 = 1/48 shutter), each frame captures just the right amount of motion blur.

The 180-Degree Shutter Rule

The 180-degree shutter rule means your shutter speed should be double your frame rate. At 24 fps, use 1/48 shutter. At 30 fps, use 1/60. This creates natural motion blur that the human eye perceives as “cinematic.” Too fast a shutter speed makes movement look jagged and “video-like.”

Most phone cameras shoot at 30 or 60 fps with auto shutter — which is why phone footage looks like phone footage. Changing these two settings alone transforms your video quality.

ISO and White Balance: Control Light and Color

Keep ISO as low as possible. ISO controls how sensitive your camera sensor is to light. Higher ISO = brighter image, but also more grain and noise. The solution? Light the room — don’t crank the ISO.

For real estate agents filming listing tours or talking-head content:

  • Natural light: Film near windows during daytime. Position yourself facing the light source.
  • Indoor filming: Use a simple ring light or LED panel. A $30 ring light makes more difference than a $1,000 camera.
  • ISO goal: Keep it under 400 whenever possible. Under 200 is ideal.

Lock your white balance. Auto white balance causes color shifting mid-video — one second your skin looks normal, the next you look orange. Lock the white balance before you start filming and don’t touch it. This ensures consistent color throughout your entire video.

Focus and Color Profile: The Final Polish

Set your focus to manual. Set it once. Don’t let the phone hunt. Auto-focus is the enemy of professional video — it constantly adjusts, creating that annoying “breathing” effect where the camera shifts focus between you and the background.

With manual focus on the Blackmagic Camera app, you tap to set your focus point, and it stays locked. You can move around the room, gesture with your hands, and your face stays perfectly sharp the entire time.

Set your profile to Flat or Film. These color profiles capture more detail in the highlights and shadows, giving you more room to color-grade in post-production. Flat profile looks “washed out” in the app, but that’s intentional — it preserves data that can be enhanced later.

If you’re not color-grading yourself (and most agents shouldn’t be), Film profile is the better choice — it looks good straight out of camera while still preserving more detail than the default “Standard” profile.

That’s it. No fancy gear. No excuses. Here’s every setting at a glance:

    Cinematic motion blur. The standard for professional video.
