Which means the phone has quietly become a Blue Ocean strategy — open, effective, and completely underused. The more agents avoid it, the more powerful it becomes for those who hire a VA for real estate appointments.

The 3-Step Process to Hire a VA for Real Estate Appointments

  1. Get Scripts That Actually Work

    Don’t wing it. Use tested scripts crafted around reconnecting with past clients, inviting people to coffee or events, offering value (not asking for business), and prospecting FSBOs, expireds, or internet leads.

  2. Hire a Professional Appointment Setter

    Sites like OnlineJobs.ph are goldmines. You can find Filipino professionals who speak fluent English, often have little to no accent, are reliable and consistent, and can represent you as your assistant or client concierge.

  3. Train Them Like They’re Local

    This is key. Teach them local slang (even your town’s nicknames), the weather that day (so they sound present), and your client base’s style, tone, and preferences. They should say: “Hi! This is Mary — I’m calling on behalf of [Agent Name], your local Realtor here in [City].”

It’s not spam. It’s client care at scale. They book coffees. They reconnect you with past clients. They follow up with leads. You walk into warm, qualified conversations.

The Exact Phone Script Your VA Should Use

When you hire a VA for real estate appointments, the script is everything. Here’s the proven format that gets results:

VA Appointment-Setting Script

“Hi! This is [VA Name] — I’m calling on behalf of [Agent Name], your local Realtor here in [City]. We’re just setting up some client coffee meetups this month and [Agent Name] wanted to reconnect and treat you to lunch. Would next Tuesday or Thursday work better for you?”

Notice what this script does: it leads with generosity (lunch/coffee), positions you as proactive, and makes the ask easy with a two-option close. Your VA isn’t selling anything — they’re creating conversations that you then walk into.

Have your VA make 15+ calls per session. The math is simple:

  • Call 15 past clients → get a lunch, a referral, or a listing
  • Call 15 warm leads → get a showing or a consult
  • Call 15 new contacts a day → 300+ conversations a month

Don’t Just Call — Add Value When You Do

When you hire a VA for real estate appointments, the tone matters as much as the words. Train your VA to:

  • Ask how the family is doing — genuine care, not a script
  • Mention a cool local event happening this weekend
  • Offer to set up a coffee date, not a “consultation”
  • Share insight about their neighborhood (home values, new developments)
  • Invite them to your next client appreciation BBQ or event

When your VA shows up as the helpful, friendly local representative — not a desperate cold-caller — people don’t avoid the call. They welcome it.
