Where to Find the Services Section on Your Google Business Profile

Most realtors don’t even know this section exists. Here’s exactly how to find it and start adding google business profile keywords immediately.

  1. Go to business.google.com

    Log into your Google Business Profile. Make sure you’re signed into the Google account that manages your business listing.

  2. Click “Edit Services”

    Look on the left side menu for the “Edit Services” option. This is where the magic happens. Most agents either skip this entirely or add 3 generic items and move on.

  3. Start Adding Keyword Phrases

    Add every keyword phrase someone might type into Google when looking for a realtor. “Realtor near me,” “find a realtor,” “best real estate agent in [City]” — include all nearby cities you serve.

Google Business Profile Services Section

The services section of your GBP is a free-text field where you can list every service your business offers. Unlike other profile fields, there is no limit on how many services you can add. Each service also has a hidden description field for even more google business profile keywords.

What Most Real Estate Agents Do Wrong with Their Services

Most realtors either skip the services section entirely or add 3 generic services and call it done. That’s a huge mistake. Here’s what the typical agent’s services section looks like:

  • Buyer Agent
  • Seller Agent
  • Property Management

Three services. That’s it. Meanwhile, Google is looking at your profile trying to figure out if you’re relevant for thousands of different search queries. With only 3 generic services listed, you’re essentially invisible for most searches.

Compare that to what the top-ranking agents have:

  • 1,000+ keyword phrases covering every search variation
  • Every city and neighborhood they serve listed as a separate service
  • Descriptions filled with additional google business profile keywords for each service
  • Niche services like “first-time buyer specialist,” “relocation agent,” “divorce real estate,” and “luxury home specialist”

The difference in ranking between 3 services and 1,000+ services is dramatic. Google sees all those keywords and your profile becomes supremely relevant to local searches.

Use Gemini to Generate 100+ Google Business Profile Keywords at a Time

Use Gemini because unlike other AI tools, it’s directly connected to Google — the largest search engine in the world. This means the keyword phrases Gemini generates are based on what people are actually searching for on Google right now.

Here’s the exact process:

  1. Open Gemini (gemini.google.com)

    Go to Gemini and type in this prompt: “Give me 100 keywords and phrases people are searching for on Google when they are looking for a realtor in [your city].”

  2. Copy the Results

    Gemini will generate 100 keyword phrases instantly. Copy the entire list. These are real search terms that real people are typing into Google.

  3. Paste Into Your Services Section

    Go back to your Google Business Profile, click Edit Services, and paste each keyword phrase as a new service. It takes 10–15 minutes to add all 100.

  4. Repeat for Every City You Serve

    Run the same prompt for every nearby city and neighborhood. If you serve 5 cities, that’s 500+ google business profile keywords from one AI session.

The Hidden Description Field Most Agents Miss

When you click on each service you’ve added, a hidden description field drops down. This is where you can add even more google business profile keywords for each individual service. Most agents never discover this because they never click on the services they’ve already added.

Here’s how to use the hidden descriptions:

  • Click on any service you’ve added — a description field will appear below it
  • Write a 2–3 sentence description loaded with keyword variations and city names
  • Include related search terms that people might use when looking for that specific service

For example, if your service is “First-Time Home Buyer Agent in Nashville,” your description could include: “Helping first-time home buyers in Nashville, Franklin, Brentwood, and Murfreesboro find their dream home. Specializing as a realtor for first-time buyers, FHA loans, and down payment assistance programs in the Nashville metro area.”

Every description is another opportunity to tell Google what you do, who you serve, and where you do it. This compounding effect is what pushes your profile from page 3 to the top 10.

Why Adding Google Business Profile Keywords Works for Local SEO

Google ranks your profile based on three factors: Proximity, Relevance, and Activity. The services section is a direct relevance signal. When you add 1,000+ google business profile keywords, you’re telling Google you’re relevant to 1,000+ search queries.

Here’s the breakdown of why this strategy is so effective:

  • Relevance: Google sees all these keywords and associates your profile with those search terms. The more keyword phrases you have, the more relevant you appear for more searches.
  • No limit: Unlike other fields on your GBP that have character limits, the services section lets you add as many entries as you want. There is literally no cap.
  • Hidden from users: The services section doesn’t clutter your public profile. Clients see a clean listing while Google sees a keyword-rich profile.
  • Compounding effect: Each service + its description creates multiple keyword signals. 100 services with descriptions gives Google 200+ relevance data points.

This one change alone can push you into the top 10 of local Google search results. Agents who maximize their services section consistently outrank competitors who leave it empty or under-optimized.
