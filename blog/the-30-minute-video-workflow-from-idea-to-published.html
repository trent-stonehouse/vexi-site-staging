The 30-Minute Video Workflow: From Idea to Published

TRENT STONEHOUSE4 MIN READAPRIL 1, 2026

⚡ Quick Answer

This system lets you create a month’s worth of content in under an hour.

Most realtors waste 20+ hours per week on content and still can’t stay consistent. The problem isn’t talent or creativity—it’s the lack of a system. This four-step workflow turns content creation from an all-day struggle into a 30-minute process.

78% of realtors say inconsistent posting is their biggest social media challenge

Source: NAR Technology Survey

The secret is batching. Instead of creating one video at a time, you generate 10 scripts at once, record them back-to-back, and upload them all in one session. While other realtors are struggling to post once a week, you’re dominating daily—and it took you less time than they spent on a single post.

Step 1: Find What People Are Searching For

  1. Go to AnswerSocrates.com

    This free tool shows you exactly what people in your market are searching for on Google. Type in a keyword like “buying a home” and set the location to your target city.

AnswerSocrates pulls the top most frequently used questions people ask in your area. These aren’t random ideas—they’re topics with proven search demand. When you create content around these questions, you’re answering what your ideal clients are already looking for.

For example, if you search “buying a home” with Nashville as your location, you might see questions like “How much do I need for a down payment in Nashville?” or “What credit score do I need to buy a home in Tennessee?” Each question is a video waiting to be recorded.

Search-Driven Content

Content created around topics people are actively searching for, rather than topics you assume they care about. Search-driven content has higher engagement because it answers questions your audience is already asking.

Run one search and you’ll get 50–100 questions. That’s months of content ideas in two minutes.

Step 2: Turn Searches Into Scripts With Claude.ai

Open Claude.ai—the best AI writing tool for realtors—and copy those search queries from AnswerSocrates. Then give Claude this simple instruction:

“Turn these into 10 short video scripts optimized for these searches.”

Done. You now have 10 scripts in two minutes. Claude writes them conversational and natural—they sound like you talking to a client, not like a robot reading a Wikipedia article. Each script includes a hook, the value content, and a call to action.

“Most realtors waste 20+ hours per week on content and still can’t stay consistent. Here’s how to create a month’s worth of videos in under an hour.”
— Trent Stonehouse, VEXI

The key is to feed Claude context about your business first. Tell it your city, your niche, your ideal client. The more context it has, the more personalized and on-brand the scripts will be. Once Claude knows your business, every script it generates speaks directly to your audience.

AI-assisted content creation reduces script writing time by 85% for real estate professionals

Step 3: Record Like a Pro With BlackMagic Camera

Download the BlackMagic Camera app—it’s completely free and gives you cinema-quality footage from your phone. No expensive camera equipment needed.

  1. Download BlackMagic Camera

    Available free on iOS. This app gives you professional-grade video settings that make your iPhone footage look like it was shot on a $5,000 camera.

  2. Adjust the Settings

    Turn on the recommended settings for crisp, professional footage. The app handles color grading, frame rate, and exposure automatically once configured.

  3. Record All 10 Videos Back-to-Back

    Set up in one spot with good lighting. Read through each script and record. Don’t overthink it—conversational delivery beats polished every time. All 10 videos should take about 30 minutes.

The batch recording approach is critical. When you set up once and record 10 videos in a row, you eliminate the biggest time waster: setup and context switching. No more “I’ll record one video today and another tomorrow.” You do it all at once and you’re done for the month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the BlackMagic Camera app?BlackMagic Camera is a free iOS app that gives you professional-grade video controls on your iPhone. It provides cinema-quality footage with advanced settings for frame rate, color grading, and exposure—making your phone videos look like they were shot on professional equipment.
How many videos can I create with this workflow in one session?Most realtors can create 10–15 videos in a single batching session of about 30–45 minutes. With the AI scripts already written, recording is fast because you’re just reading and delivering naturally. That gives you 2–3 weeks of daily content.
Do I need any paid tools for this workflow?AnswerSocrates has a free tier, Claude.ai has a free tier, and BlackMagic Camera is completely free.
What if I’m not comfortable on camera?The AI-generated scripts make it easier because you’re not improvising—you’re reading a conversational script. Start with just your voice over B-roll footage of properties or your neighborhood. As you get comfortable, transition to face-to-camera content.
Can I use this workflow for content besides Instagram Reels?Yes. The videos created through this workflow work on every short-form platform: Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, and LinkedIn video. VEXI optimizes each video for each platform’s specific requirements and posts everywhere automatically.
