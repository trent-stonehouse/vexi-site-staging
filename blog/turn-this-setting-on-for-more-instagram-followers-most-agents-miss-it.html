Turn This Setting On for More Instagram Followers (Most Agents Miss It)

The setting is only accessible through a desktop browser at instagram.com — not the mobile app. Turning it on allows Instagram to recommend your profile to users who follow accounts similar to yours, which is one of the highest-converting organic discovery channels on the platform.

The difference might be a single setting buried in Instagram’s desktop interface that most agents have never seen — and some have accidentally turned off.

The Hidden Setting Most Agents Never Find

Instagram has a toggle called “Suggest Your Account to Others” that determines whether your profile shows up in the “Suggested for You” recommendations. These are the profile cards that appear when someone follows an account similar to yours, or when Instagram populates the Explore page and feed suggestions.

The setting lives in your account’s web-based settings — not the mobile app. That’s why most agents have never seen it. If you’ve only ever managed Instagram from your phone, this toggle exists in a place you’ve likely never visited.

Here’s what makes this critical: when this setting is off, Instagram will not recommend your profile to other users. Your content still appears in hashtag searches and Explore if the algorithm picks it up, but your actual profile card — the one that says “Suggested for You” with a Follow button — does not show up. You are invisible in one of Instagram’s most powerful organic growth channels.

Accounts with the suggestion setting enabled receive an average of 2–4x more profile visits from non-followers compared to accounts with it disabled, according to Instagram growth analysts tracking business account metrics.

Source: Later, How Instagram Suggested Posts Work, 2024

The worst part? Many agents turned this off without realizing it. If you ever went through Instagram’s privacy settings and unchecked boxes to “protect your account,” you may have disabled this feature. New business accounts sometimes have it enabled by default — but not always, especially if the account was converted from a personal profile.

What Is “Suggest Your Account to Others”?

Definition

“Suggest Your Account to Others” is an Instagram account setting that allows the platform to recommend your profile to other users. When enabled, your account can appear in the “Suggested for You” section — the row of profile cards shown after someone follows a similar account, in the Explore tab, and in the main feed between posts. Disabling it removes your profile from all suggestion surfaces.

Instagram uses three primary suggestion surfaces where your profile card can appear:

  • Post-follow suggestions: When a user follows an account similar to yours, Instagram shows a row of 4–6 related accounts to follow. This is the highest-intent suggestion — the user just demonstrated interest in your category.
  • Feed suggestions: Instagram inserts “Suggested for You” blocks directly in the main feed, typically between stories from accounts the user already follows.
  • Explore tab: Your profile card (not just individual posts) can appear in the Explore grid for users who engage with content in your niche.

All three of these surfaces are disabled for your account when the “Suggest Your Account to Others” toggle is off. Your individual posts can still appear in Explore through the content algorithm, but your profile card — the one with the Follow button that makes it frictionless for someone to add you — gets pulled from the system entirely.

Instagram’s Explore and Suggestions account for over 50% of all account discovery on the platform, surpassing hashtags, location tags, and direct search combined.

Source: Instagram, How Instagram Works, 2023

Why This Setting Is Desktop-Only

This is one of the most common points of confusion. You cannot find this toggle in the Instagram mobile app. It does not exist in the app’s settings menu, privacy controls, or account preferences. It is only accessible through a web browser on desktop at instagram.com.

Instagram has historically kept certain account-level controls in the web interface rather than the mobile app. This includes data download requests, some privacy toggles, and the suggestion opt-in. The practical result is that agents who manage their Instagram entirely from their phone — which is most agents — never encounter this setting.

This is not a bug or a hidden feature. It is a standard account setting. But its placement means it has an outsized impact relative to how few people know about it. If you have never logged into Instagram on a computer, there is a good chance you have never seen or configured this option.

Important Note

You only need to change this setting once. After enabling it on desktop, it stays enabled permanently — even when you continue managing your account from the mobile app. You do not need to re-enable it. One login on a browser, one toggle, and you’re done.

How to Enable Suggested Profiles (Step by Step)

This takes under 60 seconds. Follow these steps from any desktop or laptop browser.

  1. Open Instagram in a desktop browser

    Go to instagram.com and log into your account. Use Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge — any modern browser works. Do not use the mobile app for this.

  2. Navigate to your profile

    Click your profile picture in the bottom-left sidebar (or top-right on older layouts) to go to your profile page.

  3. Open “Edit Profile”

    Click the “Edit Profile” button next to your username. This opens your profile editing panel.

  4. Find “Show account suggestions on profiles”

    Scroll down in the Edit Profile form. Look for a checkbox labeled “Show account suggestions on profiles” or “Similar Account Suggestions.” The exact wording varies slightly by account type and region.

  5. Check the box and submit

    Make sure the checkbox is checked (enabled). If it was already checked, you’re all set. If it was unchecked, check it and click “Submit” to save your changes.

  6. Verify the change

    Reload the page and return to Edit Profile. Confirm the checkbox is still checked. The change is permanent — you do not need to repeat this. Return to using the mobile app as usual.

That’s it. Six steps, under a minute, and your account is now eligible to appear in all of Instagram’s suggestion surfaces. Most agents who discover this setting report noticing increased profile visits within the first week.

How the Suggestion Algorithm Picks Accounts

Enabling the setting is step one. But Instagram does not suggest every account equally. The algorithm uses several signals to decide which profiles to recommend and how prominently to feature them.

Understanding these signals helps you optimize your profile so you appear in suggestions more frequently — and to the right people.

The Key Ranking Signals

  • Account similarity: Instagram groups accounts by behavior patterns. If users who follow Account A also tend to follow Account B, both accounts get suggested alongside each other. The more overlap in your follower base with other real estate accounts, the more often you get surfaced.
  • Engagement velocity: Accounts with strong engagement rates (likes, saves, shares, comments relative to follower count) are suggested more aggressively. Instagram treats high engagement as a quality signal.
  • Content freshness: Accounts that post regularly are prioritized over dormant ones. If you haven’t posted in two weeks, you are far less likely to appear in suggestions — even with the setting enabled.
  • Profile completeness: Accounts with a bio, profile photo, contact buttons, and linked website rank higher in suggestions. Incomplete profiles signal low quality to the algorithm.
  • Mutual connections: If the target user follows people who follow you, you get a boost. This is why local agents who are well-connected in their market tend to grow faster through suggestions.

Instagram Reels receive 2x more reach from non-followers than static posts, making Reels the single best content format for triggering the suggestion algorithm. Accounts that post Reels consistently appear in suggestions more often.

Source: Hootsuite, Instagram Statistics & Trends, 2025

What Gets You Removed from Suggestions

Instagram will suppress your suggestions if your account triggers any of the following:

  • Repeated guideline violations — even minor ones like using banned hashtags
  • Low engagement rate — accounts with lots of followers but minimal interaction signal bought followers or irrelevant content
  • Inactivity — accounts that stop posting for 30+ days get deprioritized in all recommendation systems
  • High unfollow rates — if people frequently follow you from suggestions and then quickly unfollow, Instagram interprets this as a quality problem

The setting opens the door. Your content, engagement, and profile quality determine how far you walk through it.

Why This Matters More for Real Estate Agents

Real estate is one of the most geographically concentrated industries on Instagram. Your ideal clients are not scattered randomly across the internet. They are clustered in your local market, following local businesses, local news accounts, and — critically — other local agents.

This is exactly the pattern the suggestion algorithm looks for. When someone in your city follows a competing agent, a local mortgage broker, or a neighborhood community page, Instagram looks for other local real estate accounts to suggest. If your setting is enabled and your profile is optimized, you are in that suggestion pool. If the setting is off, you are not.

97% of home buyers use the internet during their home search, and 54% of buyers under 40 found the home they purchased through social media or online search.

Source: NAR, Home Buyers and Sellers Generational Trends, 2024

Here’s the compounding effect for agents specifically:

  • Hyperlocal relevance: Unlike national brands, your value is tied to a specific geographic area. The suggestion algorithm excels at connecting users with geographically relevant accounts, which plays directly to your advantage.
  • Category clustering: Real estate content is a well-defined category on Instagram. Users who engage with one real estate account tend to engage with multiple, creating a tight suggestion loop that benefits all active agents in a market.
  • Low competition for the setting: Most agents in any given market either do not know about this toggle or have it disabled. Enabling it gives you a structural advantage over competitors who are otherwise doing similar content work.
  • Trust transfer: When your profile appears alongside an account a user already trusts and follows, you benefit from implicit credibility. The user sees your profile in a context that signals relevance and quality.

This is free organic distribution from Instagram itself. There is no ad spend, no engagement pod, no growth hack. It is a built-in platform feature that rewards accounts who opt in and maintain quality.

Your Profile Optimization Checklist

Enabling the setting only works if your profile is worth following when someone sees it. The suggestion algorithm gets people to your profile. Your profile has to close the follow. Here is what to audit.

  1. Profile photo

    Use a clear, professional headshot — not a logo, not a group photo, not a distant shot where your face is unrecognizable. People follow people, especially in real estate. Your face builds trust before they read a single word.

  2. Username and display name

    Your username should be recognizable and searchable. Your display name should include your market — for example, “Sarah Kim | Austin Realtor.” This helps both the algorithm and human visitors understand what you do and where you operate.

  3. Bio with clear value proposition

    State who you help, where you operate, and what they get by following you. Example: “Helping first-time buyers in DFW find homes under $400K. Market tips daily. DM me BUYER for my free checklist.” Skip the generic filler lines.

  4. Contact buttons enabled

    Make sure your email, phone, and address buttons are active on your business profile. These signals help Instagram categorize your account and increase your suggestion eligibility. A profile without contact methods looks incomplete to the algorithm.

  5. Category set to “Real Estate Agent”

    Your business category directly influences which accounts Instagram groups you with for suggestions. Make sure it is set to “Real Estate Agent,” “Real Estate,” or “Realtor” — not a generic category like “Entrepreneur” or “Public Figure.”

  6. Highlight covers organized

    Story Highlights are the first thing visitors scan after your bio. Create highlights for: Listings, Testimonials, Market Updates, About Me, and a CTA (free resource or consultation link). Use branded covers that match your visual identity.

  7. Recent posts are on-brand and consistent

    When a suggested user lands on your profile, they see your top 9 posts. If those posts are random, off-brand, or weeks old, they will not follow. Make sure your grid reflects your content pillars: value content, connection content, and social proof.

Think of your profile as a landing page. The suggestion algorithm is the traffic source. Your profile photo, bio, and top posts are the conversion elements. If those are weak, the setting does not matter — people will see your card, visit your profile, and leave without following.

Instagram users decide whether to follow an account within 3–5 seconds of visiting a profile. Your bio, profile photo, and top row of posts are the only things they evaluate before tapping Follow or leaving.

Source: Sprout Social, Instagram Benchmarks, 2025

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the “Suggest Your Account to Others” setting on Instagram?This setting is only available on the desktop web version of Instagram at instagram.com. Log in, go to your profile, click “Edit Profile,” and scroll down to find the “Show account suggestions on profiles” checkbox. It is not accessible through the Instagram mobile app.
Does enabling “Suggest Your Account” cost anything or require a business account?No. This feature is completely free and available to both personal and business/creator accounts. It is not a paid promotion or ad feature. It simply allows Instagram’s organic recommendation system to include your profile in suggestions shown to other users.
How long does it take to see results after enabling the setting?Most accounts notice increased profile visits within 7–14 days of enabling the setting, assuming they are also posting consistently and have a complete profile. The suggestion algorithm needs time to index your account and match it with relevant users. Accounts that post Reels regularly tend to see results faster.
Can I control which accounts Instagram suggests me alongside?Not directly. Instagram’s algorithm automatically determines which accounts to group yours with based on follower overlap, content similarity, and engagement patterns. However, you can influence this by setting your business category to “Real Estate Agent,” engaging with other real estate accounts in your market, and posting content clearly related to real estate.
Will this setting help me get more real estate clients specifically?The setting increases your visibility to users who already follow real estate-related accounts, local business accounts, and profiles similar to yours. This means the followers you gain through suggestions are more likely to be locally relevant and interested in real estate content. Combined with a strong profile and consistent content, this translates to higher-quality followers who are more likely to become leads.
