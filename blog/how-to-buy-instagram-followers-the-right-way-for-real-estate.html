Every real estate agent wants more Instagram followers. More followers means more visibility, more credibility, more leads. But most agents either grind for months hoping organic growth kicks in, or they buy cheap followers from sketchy websites and end up with 5,000 bots that destroy their engagement rate.

Instagram has a built-in paid boost feature that lets you put your best content in front of real people in your local market. When you set it up correctly — with the right objective, the right targeting, the right content, and the right budget — you can buy real Instagram followers who live in your city, are interested in real estate, and can actually become clients.

Let’s break it down.

Instagram has over 2 billion monthly active users, and 90% of users follow at least one business. For real estate agents, Instagram is the #1 platform for visual content, local discovery, and building trust with potential buyers and sellers in your market.

What “Buying Instagram Followers” Actually Means in 2026

Let’s clear this up immediately. When most people hear “buy Instagram followers,” they think of those $10-for-1,000-followers websites. That is not what this strategy is. Those services deliver fake accounts, bots, and inactive profiles from other countries. They tank your engagement rate, signal to Instagram’s algorithm that your account is low quality, and they will never buy a house from you.

When we say “buy Instagram followers the right way,” we mean using Instagram’s official boost feature to pay for real people in your local market to discover your profile. You are paying Instagram to show your best content to targeted users. Those users watch your Reel or see your post, visit your profile because they liked what they saw, and choose to follow you.

Instagram Boost vs. Buying Fake Followers

Instagram Boost is a paid advertising feature built into the Instagram app. You select a post or Reel, choose an objective (Visit Profile), set your target audience and budget, and Instagram shows that content to real users. Buying fake followers means paying a third-party website to add bot accounts to your follower list. One builds a real audience. The other destroys your account.

The key difference: boosting builds followers who chose to follow you. They saw your content, liked it, visited your profile, and made a decision. That is a warm audience. That is someone who might DM you about selling their home in six months. A bot will never do that.

Real estate agents who buy Instagram followers the right way using the boost strategy see follower growth of 50–200+ new local followers per week on a $5–$10/day budget. These are real people in your city. These are potential real estate clients for your Instagram profile. And the best part — Instagram rewards you with better organic reach because your engagement rate stays high.

The “Visit Profile” Objective: Why This Setting Changes Everything

This is the single most important setting in your Instagram boost — and most agents get it wrong.

When you boost a post on Instagram, the app asks you to choose an objective. Most agents pick “More Engagement” or “More Website Visits.” Those sound logical, but they optimize for the wrong thing. “More Engagement” gets you likes from people who tap hearts on everything but never follow. “More Website Visits” sends people away from Instagram entirely.

You want “More Profile Visits.”

When you select the Visit Profile objective, Instagram optimizes your boost to show your content to people who are most likely to tap through to your profile page. Once they land on your profile, they see your bio, your grid, your highlights, your Reels — and they make a decision to follow.

Instagram users who visit a profile are 5x more likely to follow than users who only engage with a single post in their feed. The Visit Profile objective puts your entire brand in front of potential followers — not just one piece of content.

Source: Instagram Business Blog

Think of it this way. Your individual post is a first impression. But your profile is the full pitch. When someone visits your profile and sees a grid full of local market content, client testimonials, neighborhood tours, and professional real estate content — they follow. When they only see one random post in their feed, they scroll past.

Why Visit Profile Beats Every Other Objective for Real Estate Agents

  • It builds your follower count with real, interested people — not vanity likes that disappear
  • It compounds over time — every new follower sees your future organic posts, increasing lifetime reach
  • It improves your organic algorithm performance — Instagram sees new followers engaging with your content and pushes it harder
  • It creates a pipeline — today’s new follower is next quarter’s listing appointment

Every Instagram boost you run as a real estate agent should use the Visit Profile objective. No exceptions. This is the setting that turns paid reach into permanent followers who become real estate leads in your local market.

Local Targeting: Your City, Your Radius, Your Future Clients

You are a local business. Your Instagram followers need to be local people. This sounds obvious, but most agents who boost content either leave targeting wide open (everyone in the US) or pick locations that are too broad (their entire state). Both waste money.

Here’s how to set up local targeting for your Instagram boost as a real estate agent:

  1. Set your location to your city

    Open the boost settings and set your location to the city or metro area where you work. If you serve multiple cities, pick the one where you want the most clients right now.

  2. Add a radius

    Instagram lets you set a mile radius around your target city. For most real estate agents, 15–25 miles covers your service area. If you work a hyper-local niche (one neighborhood, one zip code), tighten it to 10 miles.

  3. Exclude locations that waste budget

    If your city is near a state border or a metro area you don’t serve, exclude those locations. Every dollar should reach someone who could realistically hire you to buy or sell real estate in your market.

Why Local Targeting Matters for Real Estate Instagram Growth

A follower in your city is worth 100x more than a follower across the country. Local followers see your market updates and think “that’s my neighborhood.” They see your listings and think “I know that street.” They see your client testimonials and think “that could be me.” Local relevance is what turns Instagram followers into real estate clients.

The combination of Visit Profile objective + local city targeting is what makes this strategy so powerful for real estate agents on Instagram. You are not paying for random eyeballs. You are paying to put your profile in front of homeowners, buyers, and sellers who live within driving distance of your office.

Pack your boost targeting with your city name, surrounding neighborhoods, and local landmarks. The more locally specific your targeting, the higher quality your new Instagram followers will be for your real estate business.

Using ChatGPT to Build Interest Targeting Lists for Instagram Boosts

This is the hack that separates agents who waste money on boosts from agents who get real estate followers for pennies. Instagram lets you add interest-based targeting to your boost, but the default interests are broad and generic. “Real estate” as an interest includes everyone from HGTV watchers to commercial investors in other states.

The fix: use ChatGPT to generate hyper-specific interest targeting lists for your Instagram boost.

How to Build Your Interest List with ChatGPT

  1. Open ChatGPT and describe your ideal client

    Tell ChatGPT: “I’m a real estate agent in [your city]. Give me 30 Instagram interest targets for people who are likely to buy or sell a home in the next 12 months. Include real estate interests, financial interests, lifestyle interests, and local interests.”

  2. Review and refine the list

    ChatGPT will generate interests like: Zillow, Realtor.com, mortgage rates, home improvement, first-time homebuyer, interior design, HGTV, home loans, property investment, moving companies, home inspection, and more. Pick the ones that match your ideal client.

  3. Layer interests in your Instagram boost settings

    Add 10–15 of the best interests to your boost targeting. Instagram will show your content to people in your city who match those interests. This is how you reach first-time buyers, move-up buyers, downsizers, and sellers — not just random scrollers.

  4. Create different lists for different audiences

    Build a buyer interest list and a seller interest list. Run separate boosts with separate content for each. A first-time buyer carousel hits different than a “thinking of selling?” Reel. Match the content to the audience for maximum Instagram follower growth.

Instagram ads with interest-based targeting see 40–60% lower cost-per-result compared to broad targeting. For real estate agents, layering location + interests can bring your cost-per-profile-visit down to $0.02–$0.10.

Source: Social Insider, Instagram Ads Benchmarks, 2025

The output gives you a ready-to-use interest targeting list that makes your $5–$10/day Instagram boost budget work 3–5x harder. Every real estate agent running Instagram boosts should be using ChatGPT for interest targeting. It takes 5 minutes and saves you hundreds in wasted ad spend.

The Face-On Content Rule: What to Boost on Instagram

Not all content is worth boosting. If you boost a generic listing graphic or a stock photo carousel, you will burn through your budget and get minimal results. The content you boost needs to do one job: make someone want to visit your profile and follow you.

The rule is simple: only boost content that shows your face.

Why Face-On Content Outperforms Everything Else

  • Face-on Reels and posts get 38% more engagement than posts without a human face. People connect with people, not graphics.
  • Your face builds recognition. When a potential client sees you at an open house or in the grocery store, they think “I know that person from Instagram.” That only happens if they have seen your face repeatedly.
  • Face-on content builds trust faster. You are asking people to trust you with the biggest financial decision of their life. They need to see you, hear you, and feel your energy before they reach out.
  • Instagram’s algorithm favors Reels with faces. The platform wants authentic, creator-led content — not text graphics. Boosting face-on content gets better delivery rates from Instagram.

The Face-On Content Rule for Instagram Boosts

Before you hit “Boost Post,” ask yourself: “Does this content show my face and my personality?” If yes, boost it. If no, skip it. Boost your best-performing Reels where you are talking to the camera, walking a neighborhood, giving market tips, or sharing a client story. Never boost a static graphic or text post — those are for organic reach only.

What Content to Boost as a Real Estate Agent on Instagram

  1. Reels where you talk to the camera

    Market updates, buyer tips, seller advice, local neighborhood spotlights. You talking directly into the lens is the highest-converting content type for Instagram boosts.

  2. Reels that already performed well organically

    If a Reel got above-average views, saves, or shares organically, boost it. Instagram has already validated that people like this content. Pouring budget behind a proven winner is the smartest money you can spend.

  3. Client story Reels

    Walk through a client win. “This family was told they couldn’t buy — here’s how we made it happen.” These are social proof and personality combined. They make viewers think: I want this agent working for me.

  4. Neighborhood tour Reels

    Walk through a neighborhood in your market. Talk about the schools, restaurants, vibe, price range. Local content + your face = the perfect Instagram boost content for real estate agents looking to buy followers the right way.

The face-on content rule is non-negotiable. Your face is your brand. Your personality is your differentiator. Boost the content that showcases both, and your Instagram follower growth will compound week after week.

Budget Tips for Maximum Instagram Follower Growth

  • Run boosts continuously, not in bursts. A steady $5/day for 30 days outperforms $150 in one weekend. The algorithm learns your audience over time and gets cheaper per result.
  • Only boost your top-performing content. Check your Instagram Insights. Find the Reel with the most saves, shares, or profile visits. That is the one to boost.
  • Rotate content every 7–10 days. When a boosted post starts losing steam (cost-per-profile-visit creeps up), swap it for a fresh one.
  • Never boost and forget. Check results every 2–3 days. If cost-per-profile-visit is above $0.15, adjust targeting or swap content.

Step-by-Step: Set Up Your First Instagram Boost for Follower Growth

Here is the exact process to buy Instagram followers the right way. Follow these steps and you will have your first boost running in under 10 minutes.

  1. Switch to a Professional Account

    Go to Settings → Account → Switch to Professional Account. Choose “Business.” Select “Real Estate Agent” as your category. This unlocks the boost feature and gives you access to Instagram Insights for tracking results.

  2. Pick your best-performing face-on Reel

    Open Instagram Insights and find the Reel from the last 30 days with the most saves, shares, or profile visits. This is your winner. Do not boost your newest post — boost your proven best.

  3. Tap “Boost Post” and select “More Profile Visits”

    This is critical. Choose the Visit Profile objective. Not engagement. Not website clicks. Profile visits. This is what turns paid reach into real Instagram followers for your real estate business.

  4. Set your audience: location + interests

    Choose “Create Your Own” audience. Set location to your city with a 15–25 mile radius. Add 10–15 interests from your ChatGPT-generated list. Set age range to 25–65 (active homebuyer/seller demographics).

  5. Set budget and duration

    Start with $5/day for 7 days ($35 total). This gives Instagram enough time and budget to optimize delivery. After 7 days, check your results and decide whether to continue, increase budget, or swap content.

  6. Review and launch

    Double-check your settings: Visit Profile objective, local targeting, interest list, $5/day, 14-day duration. Hit “Boost Post.” Your content will start reaching targeted local users within a few hours.

  7. Track results and optimize

    After 3–4 days, check your boost performance in Instagram Insights. Look at profile visits, new followers gained, and cost-per-profile-visit. If cost-per-visit is under $0.10, you have a winner. Keep it running. If it is over $0.20, swap the content or tighten your interest targeting.
