Why Your Instagram Reels Get Zero Views (Fix These Settings)

TRENT STONEHOUSE5 MIN READAPRIL 1, 2026

⚡ Quick Answer

Your Instagram Reels get zero views because critical settings are turned off. Fix it by enabling search engine visibility, turning on posts and reels to stories, enabling reposts, turning on GIF comments, and — most importantly — enabling translation, lip-syncing, and closed captions on every Reel upload. These settings tell the algorithm your content is ready for global reach, not just local followers.

You’re posting Reels consistently. You’re following the trends. And still — your videos barely break double-digit views while other agents in your market are racking up thousands. The problem isn’t your content. It’s your settings.

There are hidden Instagram settings that most realtors have never touched — and they’re the reason the algorithm isn’t showing your Reels to non-followers. The fix takes 30 seconds, and once you turn these on, Instagram remembers your preferences for every future upload.

Translated captions improve accessibility and can boost engagement by up to 40%. Instagram prioritizes content that can be translated and shared globally — if translation settings are off, the algorithm limits your reach.

Source: Instagram Official Blog, Reels Updates, 2025

Step 1: Fix Your Privacy and Search Engine Settings

The first instagram reels no views fix starts in your privacy settings. Most agents have no idea that Instagram has a toggle that controls whether your content appears in search engine results. If it’s off, you’re invisible to Google — and you’re limiting the algorithm’s ability to distribute your Reels.

  1. Open Account Privacy

    Go to your Instagram profile. Tap the three lines in the top right. Tap Settings and activity. Scroll down and tap Account privacy.

  2. Enable Search Engine Results

    Make sure “Allow public photos and videos to appear in search engine results” is turned ON. This single toggle lets your real estate content show up on Google — not just inside the Instagram app.

Think about it: if someone Googles “best realtor in [your city],” your Reels could appear in the results. But only if this setting is enabled. Most agents leave it off by default and wonder why their instagram reels get no views outside their existing follower base.

Step 2: Turn On Sharing and Reuse Settings

Go back to Settings and activity. Scroll down to Sharing and reuse. You need two settings turned on here.

  1. Posts and Reels to Stories

    Turn ON “Posts and reels to stories.” This allows other users to share your Reels directly to their stories — free amplification from your audience. Every share is a signal to the algorithm that your content is worth distributing further.

  2. Reposts on Posts and Reels

    Turn ON “Reposts on posts and reels.” This lets people repost your content to their feed, which extends your reach to entirely new audiences you’d never reach organically.

Then go back to Settings and tap Comments. Turn on Allow GIF Comments. GIF comments increase engagement signals on your Reels — every comment, even a GIF, tells the algorithm people are interacting with your content.

Why Sharing Settings Matter

Instagram’s algorithm weighs shares as one of the highest engagement signals — even above likes and comments. When sharing settings are disabled, you’re literally blocking the most powerful distribution mechanism the platform offers.

Step 3: The Translation Settings Most Realtors Miss

This is the big one. The instagram reels no views fix that makes the biggest difference — and you can only change it when uploading a Reel.

Next time you upload a Reel, before you hit “Share,” tap “More options” at the bottom. Scroll down to “Accessibility and translation.”

  1. Enable Translate Reel

    Turn ON “Translate reel.” Then tap Translation settings and turn ON “Include lip-syncing.”

  2. Select Translation Languages

    Tap “Translate to” and select 9 languages — Spanish, English, Portuguese, Hindi, Bangla, Marathi, Telugu, and Tamil. This tells Instagram your content is ready for global distribution.

  3. Enable Closed Captions

    Turn ON “Enable closed captions” and “Translate closed captions.” Captions improve accessibility for viewers with hearing impairments and boost engagement significantly.

Instagram prioritizes content that can be translated and shared globally. If translation settings are off, the algorithm treats your Reel as local-only content. Turn them on and Instagram will push your Reels to international buyers, out-of-state investors, and audiences who don’t even speak your language yet.

Source: Instagram Creators Blog, 2025

The good news? Once you turn these on, Instagram remembers — you won’t have to do it every time. Set it once and every future Reel gets the benefit.

Why These Settings Are the Difference Between 50 and 5,000 Views

Here’s the psychology of the algorithm: Instagram wants content that’s shareable, accessible, and translatable. When those settings are off, the algorithm interprets it as “this creator only wants a small, local audience.” It respects that — by showing your Reels to almost nobody.

When those settings are ON, you’re signaling to Instagram:

  • My content is ready for search engines — Google can index it
  • My content is shareable — other users can repost and story-share it
  • My content is accessible — closed captions for hearing-impaired viewers
  • My content is global — translated into 9+ languages with lip-syncing
  • My content encourages interaction — GIF comments are enabled

That’s a completely different signal. And the algorithm responds by pushing your content to exponentially more people — non-followers, out-of-state buyers, investors, and relocation clients who would never find you otherwise.

The Complete Instagram Reels Settings Checklist

Here’s every setting you need to check right now to fix your instagram reels no views problem. Open Instagram and work through this list — it takes less than two minutes.

  • Account Privacy → Allow public content in search engine results: ON
  • Sharing and Reuse → Posts and reels to stories: ON
  • Sharing and Reuse → Reposts on posts and reels: ON
  • Comments → Allow GIF comments: ON
  • Reel Upload → Translate reel: ON
  • Reel Upload → Include lip-syncing: ON
  • Reel Upload → Translate to 9 languages: Selected
  • Reel Upload → Enable closed captions: ON
  • Reel Upload → Translate closed captions: ON

Once these are all enabled, your Reels are optimized for maximum algorithmic distribution.

When these are disabled, Instagram’s algorithm limits your content to a tiny local audience instead of distributing it broadly.
How do I fix Instagram Reels that get no views?Go to Settings → Account Privacy and enable search engine visibility. Then enable sharing and reuse settings, allow GIF comments, and — most importantly — turn on translation, lip-syncing, and closed captions when uploading your next Reel. These settings tell Instagram your content is ready for global distribution.

Does translating my Reels actually get more views?Yes. Instagram prioritizes content that can be translated and shared globally.
