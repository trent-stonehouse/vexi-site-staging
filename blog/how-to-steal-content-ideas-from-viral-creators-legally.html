How to Steal Content Ideas from Viral Creators (Legally)

TRENT STONEHOUSE5 MIN READAPRIL 1, 2026

⚡ Quick Answer

To steal content ideas from viral creators legally, follow a three-step process: find creators with proven viral success (look for videos with real view counts, not small accounts), feed the audio into Claude.ai with a specific prompt that rewrites the script for your brand, and record your own version using the AI-generated script. You’re not copying—you’re stacking proven formats, adding your own strategy, and staying on-brand.

Most agents waste hours trying to “think” of content ideas from scratch. That’s the slowest, hardest way to create. The fastest way? Find what’s already working for other creators and model it with your own expertise. This isn’t copying—it’s strategic content creation.

Realtors who post content consistently are 3x more likely to generate inbound leads than those who don’t

Source: NAR Research

This process takes 10 minutes. You find a viral video, feed it to AI, and get back a fully customized script written for your brand, your audience, and your market. No guesswork. No staring at a blank screen. Just proven formats with your spin.

Step 1: Find Creators With Proven Viral Success

The first step is finding creators or other realtors who consistently produce viral content. Not small accounts with 200 followers. Not random posts. You want videos with real views—proof that the format, hook, and structure actually work.

  1. Search for Top Creators in Your Niche

    Look for real estate creators, mortgage brokers, or even lifestyle influencers whose content structure would work for your audience. Focus on accounts with videos that have 50K+ views consistently.

  2. Identify Outlier Videos

    Look for videos that performed significantly better than the creator’s average. A video with 500K views on an account that usually gets 10K? That’s an outlier. That format hit a nerve. That’s what you want to model.

  3. Save Them to a Folder

    Create a saved folder on Instagram or a bookmark folder for TikTok. Build a library of 20–30 proven viral videos you can model over the next month.

The key word here is “proven.” You’re not guessing what might work. You’re studying what already did. This eliminates the biggest bottleneck in content creation: coming up with ideas that actually perform.

Step 2: Use Claude.ai to Rewrite the Script for Your Brand

This is where AI turns a viral video into your own original content in under two minutes. Take the audio from the viral video and give Claude.ai this specific prompt:

The Viral Content Rewrite Prompt

“Transcribe this Instagram Reel audio and rewrite it based on everything you know about me and my business as a realtor. Use a strong hook and include a retention hook in the middle (including reverse or negative hooks if helpful). Keep it conversational and authority-driven. Align it with my brand (local visibility, real clients, conversation-first marketing). End with a natural CTA that encourages follows and saves.”

Here’s the process: hold your phone up to Claude.ai, let it transcribe the audio, and then it turns it into your own take using everything it knows about you, your business, and your audience. The output is a completely original script that uses the proven structure of the viral video but speaks in your voice about your market.

Claude.ai is the recommended tool here because it writes the most natural, conversational scripts. It doesn’t sound robotic. It sounds like you—if you had a copywriter on staff.

Step 3: Record Your Version and Post It

You now have a script that’s based on a proven viral format but customized for your brand. All that’s left is recording and posting.

  1. Record the Video

    Use your phone. No fancy equipment needed. Read the Claude-generated script naturally—it’s written in your voice, so it should feel like a conversation, not a performance.

  2. Keep It Under 90 Seconds

    Short-form content performs best between 30–90 seconds. The AI script is already optimized for this length. Don’t pad it with filler.

  3. Post Across All Platforms

    One video, every platform. Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels, LinkedIn. Repurpose once, distribute everywhere.

This entire process—finding the viral video, running it through AI, recording your version—takes about 10 minutes. That’s it. You just created a piece of content with a dramatically higher chance of performing because it’s built on a format that already proved itself.

Why This Isn’t Copying—It’s Smart Strategy

Every successful creator models what works. Music producers sample beats. Filmmakers study shot compositions. Comedians riff on similar premises. Content creation is no different. You’re not stealing someone’s video—you’re studying a format and applying it with your own expertise.

Here’s the difference between copying and modeling:

  • Copying: downloading someone’s video and reposting it as your own
  • Modeling: studying the hook structure, pacing, and format of a viral video, then creating your own version about your market with your insights

When you use Claude.ai to rewrite the script, the output is 100% original content. Different words, different examples, different CTA. The only thing you borrowed was the format—and formats aren’t copyrightable. This is exactly what the top producers do. They study what works and reproduce it with their own market knowledge.

When you do this consistently, your page will grow three to five times faster. Your engagement will skyrocket. And you’ll send way more quality leads to your business.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it legal to model content from other creators?Yes. Modeling a format or structure is completely legal. You’re not downloading or reposting their video. You’re studying the hook, structure, and pacing, then creating original content with your own expertise and voice. Formats and ideas are not copyrightable—only the specific expression is.
Why Claude.ai instead of ChatGPT for rewriting scripts?Claude.ai produces more natural, conversational scripts that sound human rather than robotic. It’s particularly strong at maintaining your brand voice and writing hooks that feel authentic. It’s the recommended AI writing tool for real estate content creation.
How many viral videos should I model per week?Start with 3–5 per week. Build a saved folder of 20–30 viral videos and work through them over the month. This gives you a steady pipeline of content ideas backed by proven performance data.
What if I can’t find viral real estate content to model?Look beyond real estate. Financial advisors, mortgage brokers, insurance agents, and even lifestyle creators use formats that transfer perfectly to real estate content. The hook structure and pacing work across niches—you just swap in your expertise.
How long does this entire process take?About 10 minutes from finding the viral video to having a finished, ready-to-record script. Finding the video takes 3 minutes, running it through Claude.ai takes 2 minutes, and reviewing the output takes 5 minutes. Recording is another 5–10 minutes on top of that.
