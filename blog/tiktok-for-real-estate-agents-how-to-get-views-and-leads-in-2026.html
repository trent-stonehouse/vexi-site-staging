TikTok for Real Estate Agents: How to Get Views and Leads in 2026

TRENT STONEHOUSE14 MIN READMARCH 31, 2026

⚡ Quick Answer

TikTok is the single best platform for real estate agents starting from zero. Unlike Instagram or Facebook, TikTok’s algorithm pushes your content to people who have never heard of you — even if you have zero followers. Use TikTok Creator Search Insights to find exactly what homebuyers in your market are searching for, then use the Content Gap feature to find topics with high demand but low competition. Agents who post 3–5 TikToks per week using search-optimized captions are generating buyer and seller leads within 30–60 days.

Every real estate coach tells you to “get on social media.” But most of them are still teaching an Instagram-first strategy. That made sense in 2022. It does not make sense in 2026.

TikTok has fundamentally changed how people discover content — and how they discover real estate agents. The platform does not care how many followers you have. It cares whether your content answers a question someone is actively searching for.

This guide covers the exact tools, content types, and strategies that are working for real estate agents on TikTok right now — including two features most agents have never heard of.

Over 40% of Gen Z uses TikTok as a search engine instead of Google. For real estate, that means homebuyers are searching “best neighborhoods in Austin” and “first time home buyer tips” directly on TikTok — and finding agents who answer those queries.

Source: Google Internal Research, 2025

Why TikTok Is the Best Platform for New Real Estate Agents

⚡ Quick Answer

TikTok’s algorithm is interest-based, not follower-based. A brand-new account with zero followers can get 10,000+ views on its first video if the content matches what people are searching for. No other platform gives new agents this kind of reach.

Every other platform punishes you for being new. Instagram shows your Reel to your existing followers first. Facebook prioritizes pages with history and engagement. LinkedIn favors established creators with large networks.

TikTok does not care about any of that. Its algorithm evaluates every single video independently. Your first TikTok gets the same chance as your 500th. That is a massive advantage if you are an agent who is just starting to build an audience.

How TikTok’s Algorithm Actually Works

When you post a video, TikTok shows it to a small test audience — typically 200–500 people. If those viewers watch most of the video, like it, comment, or share it, TikTok pushes it to a larger audience. This cycle repeats. A video that resonates can go from 500 views to 50,000 views in 24 hours.

The key signals TikTok measures:

  • Watch time — How much of the video did people watch? Completion rate is the #1 ranking factor.
  • Re-watches — Did viewers loop the video? This signals high value.
  • Shares — Shares to DMs and other platforms signal the content is worth spreading.
  • Comments — Comments (especially questions) tell TikTok the content sparked engagement.
  • Search relevance — If your caption and on-screen text match what people search, TikTok surfaces your video in search results.

Why This Matters for Real Estate

Real estate content is inherently local and specific. When someone searches “moving to Tampa pros and cons” on TikTok, the algorithm wants to show them a local agent’s video — not a national brand. You are the answer TikTok is looking for. You just need to create the content that matches the search.

TikTok videos from accounts with under 1,000 followers receive an average of 3x more views per video compared to the same content posted on Instagram Reels. The platform actively distributes content from small creators to keep the feed fresh.

Source: Later, TikTok vs Instagram Reach Study, 2025

TikTok Creator Search Insights: Your Secret Weapon for Content Ideas

⚡ Quick Answer

TikTok Creator Search Insights is a free, built-in tool that shows you exactly what people are searching for on TikTok — including search volume and trending topics. For real estate agents, this eliminates guessing. You can see that “first time home buyer tips 2026” has high search volume and create a video that directly answers it.

Most agents are still guessing what to post. They scroll through their feed, see what other agents are doing, and copy it. That is a losing strategy because you are always behind.

TikTok Creator Search Insights flips this completely. Instead of guessing, you search for your topic and TikTok tells you:

  • What exact phrases people are searching for
  • Whether search volume is high, medium, or low
  • Whether the topic is trending up or stable
  • Related searches that you can build content around

How to Use Creator Search Insights

  1. Open TikTok Creator Tools

    Go to your TikTok profile, tap the menu (three lines), and select Creator Tools. Then tap “Creator Search Insights.” This is available to all creator accounts — you do not need a certain follower count.

  2. Search Your Topic

    Type in a broad topic like “real estate,” “home buying,” or your city name. TikTok will show you a list of related searches ranked by volume and trend direction.

  3. Filter by Category

    Narrow results by selecting categories relevant to real estate: “Home & Living,” “Finance,” or “Lifestyle.” This filters out irrelevant results and surfaces the searches your target audience is making.

  4. Build Your Content Calendar Around Search Data

    Pick 5–10 high-volume searches and create one video per search query. Use the exact search phrase in your caption, on-screen text, and spoken words. This is TikTok SEO — and it is how you get discovered by people who are actively looking for what you offer.

“Stop creating content based on what you think people want to see. Start creating content based on what TikTok tells you people are actively searching for. The data is free. Use it.”

The Content Gap Feature: High Demand, Low Competition Topics

⚡ Quick Answer

The Content Gap feature inside Creator Search Insights highlights topics where search demand is high but existing content supply is low. For real estate agents, these are gold mines — you can create videos on topics with proven demand and almost no competition, giving you a massive advantage in TikTok’s search results.

This is the feature that most agents completely miss. Inside Creator Search Insights, TikTok flags certain searches with a “Content Gap” label. This means lots of people are searching for this topic, but very few creators have made videos about it.

Think about what that means. If 50,000 people per month search “cost of living in Scottsdale 2026” but only 3 videos exist on the topic, your video has almost zero competition. TikTok wants to fill that gap — and it will push your content to fill it.

What Is a Content Gap?

A content gap occurs when user search demand for a topic exceeds the supply of videos covering it. TikTok identifies these gaps automatically and labels them inside Creator Search Insights. Creating content for a content gap is the fastest way to get views on TikTok because the algorithm actively distributes videos that fill unmet search demand.

How to Find Content Gaps in Real Estate

  1. Search Your City or Market

    Type your city name into Creator Search Insights. Look for any result tagged with the “Content Gap” label. These are your highest-priority content topics.

  2. Search Broad Real Estate Topics

    Try searches like “mortgage,” “home buying,” “real estate investing,” and “home renovation.” Content gaps often appear in sub-topics that national creators overlook but local buyers care deeply about.

  3. Prioritize Local + Specific

    The more specific and local the content gap, the better. “Best school districts in Raleigh NC” is a much better content gap to fill than “how to buy a house” because your local expertise makes you the most credible answer.

Videos created for content gap topics receive 3–5x more impressions in TikTok search results compared to videos on saturated topics. Filling a content gap is the equivalent of ranking for a low-competition keyword in Google SEO.

Source: TikTok Newsroom, Creator Search Insights Launch, 2024

The agents who dominate TikTok are not the ones with the fanciest cameras or the best editing. They are the ones who answer questions nobody else is answering. The Content Gap feature tells you exactly which questions those are.

Content Types That Get Views and Leads on TikTok

⚡ Quick Answer

The four content types that perform best for real estate agents on TikTok are: neighborhood tours (highest save rate), market updates (highest share rate), home tips and education (highest comment rate), and day-in-the-life content (highest follow rate). Mix all four into your weekly schedule.

Not all TikTok content is created equal. Some formats consistently outperform others for real estate agents. Here is what works — and why.

The 4 Content Pillars for Real Estate TikTok

  1. Neighborhood Tours

    Walk through a neighborhood and highlight what makes it special — schools, restaurants, parks, vibe, price range. These videos get the highest save rate because people bookmark them for later when they are actively researching where to move. Use the neighborhood name in your caption for search.

  2. Market Updates

    Share local market data in plain English. “Here is what happened in the Denver housing market this month.” Inventory numbers, median prices, days on market. Keep it under 60 seconds. These get shared because people send them to friends and family who are thinking about buying or selling.

  3. Home Tips and Education

    Answer common questions: “What credit score do you need to buy a house?” “What are closing costs?” “Should you waive the inspection?” These generate the most comments because viewers ask follow-up questions — which is exactly how you start conversations that lead to clients.

  4. Day-in-the-Life Content

    Show what your actual day looks like. Showings, client meetings, offer negotiations, open houses. This content has the highest follow rate because it makes viewers feel like they know you. People hire agents they feel connected to — and this is how you build that connection at scale.

Content Format Tips

  • Hook in the first 2 seconds — Start with the payoff, not the setup. “This neighborhood is the best-kept secret in Dallas” beats “Hey guys, today I want to talk about…”
  • Use on-screen text — Many viewers watch with sound off. Your key points need to be visible as text overlays.
  • Keep it under 90 seconds — Shorter videos have higher completion rates, and completion rate is the #1 ranking signal on TikTok.
  • End with a CTA — “Follow for more [city] real estate tips” or “Comment your city and I’ll give you a market update.”
“You do not need professional equipment. Your phone, natural lighting, and a genuine answer to a real question will outperform a $5,000 camera setup with nothing useful to say.”

TikTok vs Instagram: Different Algorithms, Different Strategies

⚡ Quick Answer

TikTok is a discovery platform — it shows your content to strangers. Instagram is a relationship platform — it shows your content to people who already follow you. Use TikTok to get found. Use Instagram to stay top of mind. They are complementary, not interchangeable.

One of the biggest mistakes agents make is treating TikTok and Instagram identically. They create a Reel, cross-post it to TikTok, and wonder why one performs and the other flops. The algorithms are fundamentally different.

Factor TikTok Instagram Reels
Primary audience Strangers (discovery-based) Existing followers first
Key ranking signal Watch time + completion rate Engagement from followers
New account reach High — algorithm is interest-based Low — need followers to gain traction
Search function Strong — used as a search engine Growing but still secondary
Content lifespan Weeks to months (evergreen search) 48–72 hours (feed-based decay)
Best content type Educational, search-optimized Entertaining, trend-driven
Ideal length 30–90 seconds 15–60 seconds
Caption style Keyword-rich, search-optimized Conversational, hashtag-heavy
Best for Getting discovered by new leads Nurturing and converting warm leads

The smart play is to use both platforms for their strengths. TikTok is your top-of-funnel machine — it puts you in front of people who have never heard of you. Instagram is your middle-of-funnel machine — it keeps you top of mind with people who already know you.

TikTok videos appear in search results for months after posting, while Instagram Reels typically stop generating new views after 48–72 hours. For real estate agents, this means a single TikTok about “best neighborhoods in Phoenix” can generate leads for 6+ months.

Source: Buffer, TikTok Marketing Guide, 2025

The takeaway: do not abandon Instagram. But if you are starting from scratch and need to build an audience fast, TikTok gives you the biggest return on every video you create.

Cross-Posting Strategy: Film Once, Post to TikTok, Reels, and Shorts

⚡ Quick Answer

Film your video once on your phone. Post the original to TikTok first (it rewards native content). Then remove the TikTok watermark and repost to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels. One video becomes 4+ pieces of content across 4+ platforms — without extra filming time.

The #1 objection agents have about TikTok is time. “I am already struggling to keep up with Instagram. I do not have time for another platform.”

You do not need more time. You need a better system. The cross-posting strategy means you create content once and distribute it everywhere.

The Cross-Posting Workflow

  1. Film Vertically on Your Phone

    Use 9:16 aspect ratio. Keep text and your face in the center third of the frame so nothing gets cut off on any platform. Natural lighting, eye-level camera angle.

  2. Edit in CapCut

    Add captions (auto-generated), trim dead space, add on-screen text for key points. Keep the editing clean — you do not need fancy transitions. Export without a watermark.

  3. Post to TikTok First

    TikTok rewards native content. Post it there first with a search-optimized caption. Add relevant hashtags and location tags. Wait at least 2–4 hours before posting elsewhere.

  4. Repost to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Facebook

    Use the watermark-free version. Adjust captions for each platform — Instagram captions can be longer and more conversational, YouTube Shorts descriptions should include keywords. Do not post the TikTok-watermarked version to Instagram — the algorithm penalizes it.

Local Targeting and TikTok SEO for Real Estate

⚡ Quick Answer

TikTok SEO means optimizing your videos to appear in TikTok’s search results. For real estate agents, this involves: using city/neighborhood names in captions, adding location tags, including searchable keywords in on-screen text, and speaking those keywords out loud in the video. TikTok reads your captions, your text overlays, and your spoken words to determine what your video is about.

TikTok is becoming a search engine. And just like Google, the agents who understand SEO will dominate the agents who do not.

The difference is that TikTok SEO is still in its early stages. Most agents have no idea it exists. That means the opportunity is wide open right now — but it will not stay that way.

The 5 TikTok SEO Signals for Real Estate

  1. Caption Keywords

    Write your caption like a search query answer. Instead of “Check out this awesome house!” write “3 things to know before buying a home in Tampa FL in 2026.” TikTok indexes every word in your caption.

  2. On-Screen Text

    Add text overlays with your target keyword. If your video is about “best neighborhoods in Charlotte,” put that exact phrase on screen in the first 3 seconds. TikTok’s OCR (optical character recognition) reads on-screen text and uses it for search ranking.

  3. Spoken Keywords

    Say your target keyword out loud in the video. TikTok transcribes audio and uses speech-to-text for search indexing. If you say “best neighborhoods in Charlotte” in the first 5 seconds, TikTok knows exactly what your video covers.

  4. Location Tags

    Add your city or neighborhood as a location tag when posting. This tells TikTok to prioritize showing your content to users in and around that area. Combined with local hashtags like #CharlotteRealEstate or #TampaHomes, this creates strong local targeting.

  5. Hashtag Strategy

    Use a mix of broad and local hashtags. 2–3 broad (#realestate, #firsttimehomebuyer, #housetour) and 2–3 local (#DallasRealEstate, #DallasHomes, #DFWRealtor). Do not use more than 5–6 total. Overloading hashtags dilutes your signal.

TikTok search results now include a dedicated “Search” tab in the app, and videos optimized with keyword captions appear in search results for months. Real estate agents who optimize for TikTok search report that 30–50% of their views come from search rather than the For You Page.
