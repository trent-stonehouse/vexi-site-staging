The Boost Bus Strategy is a facebook boost strategy local that puts your face on every feed in your city. Boost videos (never graphics) for $50–$100 per post, targeting “People in My Local Area” by zip code and homeowner interests. Your content shows up everywhere — whether people are looking for a realtor or not. It’s like putting your face on a bus ad, but better and cheaper.

Here’s Trent Stonehouse’s favorite analogy from Your One Conversation Away…: Boosting a video is like putting your face on an ad on the side of a city bus — but better. When you boost a post on Instagram or Facebook and select “People in My Local Area,” you’re telling Meta: “Drive this bus through every feed in town.”

The facebook boost strategy local is the simplest, most cost-effective way to become unavoidable in your market. And it’s the strategy that turned agents like Chris Dinnell into household names in their cities.

The Chris Dinnell Example: How a Facebook Boost Strategy Local Made Him Famous

Chris Dinnell is a Realtor in Richmond, BC who nailed this facebook boost strategy local. He didn’t have a massive following or a fancy production team. What he had was strategy.

He created short videos about his own backyard — not the national market. He walked the streets of Richmond, featured local cafés, neighborhoods, events, and businesses. He gave people a reason to care about where they live, not about him.

Then he did what most agents skip: he boosted every single video. Just $50–$100 per post. Targeted by zip code, radius, and basic homeowner interests. The content wasn’t built to go viral. It was built to show his face to the right people — repeatedly.

“People started saying: ‘I see you everywhere.’ ‘You’re that guy from Instagram.’ That’s not branding. That’s market share — on a subconscious level.”
— Trent Stonehouse, Your One Conversation Away…

Chris’s facebook boost strategy local was simple: create local content, boost it to the right audience, and keep showing up until people think of him before they think of real estate.

The Boost Bus Strategy: Your Facebook Boost Strategy Local Playbook

Here’s how the facebook boost strategy local works step by step:

  1. Create a Video with Your Face in It

    This is non-negotiable. People don’t buy from logos. They buy from faces. If your face isn’t on it, it’s not getting boosted. Boost videos, boost personality, boost connection.

  2. Select “People in My Local Area”

    When boosting on Instagram or Facebook, select this audience option. You’re telling Meta: drive this Boost Bus through every feed in town. Your facebook boost strategy local depends on geographic targeting.

  3. Set Budget at $50–$100 Per Post

    Keep daily budget between $15–$30 per day. Run for 5–7 days. Then boost a different piece of content the next week. This isn’t about one big spend — it’s about consistent local visibility.

  4. Target by Zip Code and Homeowner Interests

    Narrow your audience to your service area. Set age between 30–55. Add relevant interests. The more targeted your facebook boost strategy local, the more efficient your spend.
