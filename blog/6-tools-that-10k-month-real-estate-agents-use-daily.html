Tool #1: Matchmaker.fm — Get Booked on Podcasts

Want to be on podcasts? Matchmaker.fm lets you swipe, get booked on shows, record once, and clip everything. It’s like a dating app for podcasts — you create a profile with your interests and expertise, and the platform matches you with hosts looking for guests like you.

Why this matters for real estate agents:

  • One podcast = 10–15 content clips for your social media
  • Backlinks on Spotify, Apple, YouTube boost your Google ranking
  • AI search tools scrape podcast transcriptions, so you show up when people ask AI for a realtor in your city
  • Instant authority — when people Google you and see podcast features, you’re no longer “just another agent”

Swipe. Get booked. Record. Send footage to VEXI to handle the editing and posting.

Tool #2: Gamma.app — Presentations, Buyer Guides, Listings

Presentations, buyer guides, listings, decks? Gamma.app turns your ideas into finished assets fast. Type what you need and it builds fully designed, client-ready presentations in minutes — not hours.

What real estate agents create with Gamma:

  • Listing presentations that close sellers on the spot
  • Buyer guides that position you as the neighborhood expert
  • Market reports with charts and data visualization
  • Open house one-pagers with property highlights and your branding
  • Neighborhood guides breaking down schools, restaurants, and amenities

The best part: you can turn any presentation into a live website link you text to clients. No PDFs. No attachments. Just click and view. Your clients see a polished, professional asset — and you made it in 5 minutes.

Tool #3: Nano Banana — AI Visuals and Creative Graphics

AI visuals and creative graphics? Nano Banana gives you quick inspiration without overthinking. When you need a custom visual for a social post, a listing ad, or a market update graphic, Nano Banana generates options fast.

This tool fills the gap between “I need a graphic” and “I don’t have time to design one.” Instead of opening Canva and staring at templates, describe what you want and let AI generate it. Real estate agents use it for:

  • Social media post graphics with custom imagery
  • Ad creative variations for testing
  • Visual concepts for listing marketing
  • Quick inspiration when creative blocks hit

Tools #4 & #5: Instantly.ai and AnswerSocrates

Cold email at scale? Instantly.ai — thousands of leads quickly. This tool handles the infrastructure for outbound email campaigns: domain warming, email deliverability, and automated sequences that land in inboxes (not spam folders).

For agents who want to proactively reach potential sellers — like homeowners who haven’t listed yet or investors in specific neighborhoods — Instantly.ai lets you run professional outbound campaigns without the technical headache.

Content ideas people are actually searching? AnswerSocrates — real questions, real demand. Type a keyword like “buying a home in Nashville” and AnswerSocrates shows you the exact questions people in your area are asking. These become your content topics, your video scripts, and your blog posts.

The combination is powerful: AnswerSocrates tells you what your market is searching for, and you create content around those exact questions. Your content matches real demand, which means more views, more engagement, and more leads.

Tool #6: BrandPush.com — Press, Backlinks, and SEO

Press, backlinks, and SEO juice? BrandPush.com — credibility that compounds. BrandPush gets your name and story published on news sites and industry publications, creating high-authority backlinks that boost your Google ranking over time.

Why backlinks matter for real estate agents:

  • Google trusts sites that are mentioned on authoritative domains. A link from a news publication tells Google you’re a credible authority.
  • AI search tools prioritize agents with strong online presence. When ChatGPT or Perplexity is asked to recommend a realtor, agents with press mentions and backlinks surface first.
  • Credibility compounds. One press mention leads to more interview requests, podcast invitations, and speaking opportunities. Each one creates more backlinks.

BrandPush handles the outreach and placement. You provide your story and expertise; they get it published. Combined with podcast guesting (Matchmaker.fm), you build an online presence that makes you the obvious choice when someone Googles “realtor in [your city].”
