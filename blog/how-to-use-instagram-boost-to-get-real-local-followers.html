How to Use Instagram Boost to Get Real Local Followers

TRENT STONEHOUSE5 MIN READAPRIL 1, 2026

⚡ Quick Answer

To get real local followers with Instagram boost: choose “Visit Your Profile” as the goal (not website or messages), select a Local audience with your city as the radius, set age to 30–60, add interests using ChatGPT suggestions, and spend $20/day for 5–7 days. Next week, boost a different post. Always boost videos or graphics with your face — people follow people, not logos.

Going viral won’t grow your business. Local followers will. The agents who are growing fastest on Instagram aren’t chasing millions of views — they’re spending $20/day to put their face in front of every homeowner in their city.

When you boost a post on Instagram the right way, you’re not buying fake followers. You’re buying visibility with the exact people who might hire you: local homeowners, first-time buyers, and investors in your market.

Instagram boosted posts with geo-targeting see 4x higher follower conversion rates than broad-audience boosts. Local targeting ensures every dollar goes toward reaching people who could actually become your clients.

Step 1: Choose “Visit Your Profile” as the Goal

First — choose Visit Your Profile. Not website. Not messages. You want profile visits because that’s how followers happen.

When someone clicks through to your profile from a boosted post, they see your bio, your content grid, and your story highlights. If your profile is dialed in (and it should be), they hit Follow. That’s the conversion path: boosted post → profile visit → follow.

Choosing “Website” sends people off Instagram entirely. Choosing “Messages” is too aggressive for cold audiences. Profile visits create the perfect middle ground: low-commitment for the viewer, high-value for you.

Why Profile Visits Matter

A profile visit is the highest-intent action before a follow. When someone visits your profile, they’re evaluating whether to add you to their feed. A well-optimized profile with consistent local content converts these visits into followers at 15–25% rates.

Step 2: Set Your Audience to Local (Not Regional)

Click Audience. Choose Local, not regional. Turn ON your current location. Set the radius to your actual city. This forces Instagram to show your content to people who physically live near you.

Here’s why “Local” beats “Regional”:

  • Regional might show your content to someone 3 hours away who will never hire you
  • Local means every impression goes to someone who could bump into you at the grocery store
  • Local followers engage more — they care about your market updates, your neighborhood tips, your listings

Then set the age range to 30–60. Those are the buyers. Not teens. Not doom-scrollers. Adults who own homes, want to buy homes, or need to sell homes. Every dollar you spend reaches someone in the right life stage to need a real estate agent.

Step 3: Use ChatGPT to Find the Right Interests

If you don’t know what interests to add, use ChatGPT. Ask: “What interests does a home buyer / seller / investor have?” Then add those interests to your boost settings.

ChatGPT will suggest interests like:

  • Home improvement, interior design, HGTV
  • Real estate investing, property management
  • Mortgage rates, first-time home buying
  • Local business pages, community events
  • Family activities, school districts, parenting

These interest layers ensure Instagram shows your boosted post to people who aren’t just in your city — they’re in your city AND interested in topics related to buying, selling, or owning a home. That’s precision targeting for $20/day.

Step 4: $20/Day, 5–7 Days, Then Rotate

Daily spend: $20 a day. Run it for 5–7 days. Next week — boost a different post. Fresh content. New eyeballs. Same city.

Here’s why rotation matters:

  • Ad fatigue is real: The same post shown to the same people for 3 weeks loses impact. Weekly rotation keeps everything fresh.
  • Variety builds authority: One week they see your market update, next week a listing tour, then a client success story. You’re not a one-trick agent — you’re the local expert on everything real estate.
  • New content reaches new people: Instagram’s algorithm serves different content to different users. Rotating posts expands your total reach within the same city.

The monthly investment: roughly $500–$600. That’s less than one Zillow lead that ghosts you. And instead of a cold lead, you’re building an audience of local people who see your face every week and think of you when they need an agent.

Always Boost Content with Your Face

Always boost a video or a graphic with your face on it. People don’t follow logos. They follow people.

When a homeowner scrolls past your boosted post and sees YOUR face — not a stock photo, not a branded graphic with no human element — something clicks. They see you as a real person, not a business. That emotional connection is what drives the follow, the DM, and eventually the listing appointment.

Types of content to boost:

  • Talking-head videos where you share local market insights
  • Listing walkthroughs with you on camera
  • Graphics with your headshot alongside tips or stats
  • Client success story videos where you tell the story

Never boost: text-only posts, stock photos, logo-only graphics, or memes. These don’t build personal brand recognition. You’re investing to make people recognize YOUR face in your market.

What’s the difference between Instagram boost and Instagram ads?Boosts are simplified ads you create directly from a post. They’re faster to set up and designed for engagement and profile visits. Full Instagram ads (through Meta Ads Manager) offer more targeting options but require more setup. For local real estate agents building followers, boosts are simpler and equally effective.
Should I choose “Visit Profile” or “Website” for my Instagram boost?Choose “Visit Your Profile” for follower growth. Profile visits lead to follows. Website traffic sends people off Instagram, which breaks the follow conversion path. Save website goals for specific landing pages with lead magnets.
How do I target only local people with Instagram boost?When setting up your boost, choose “Local” for audience location (not regional). Turn on your current location and set the radius to your city. Set age to 30–60 and add real estate-related interests using ChatGPT suggestions. This ensures every impression reaches a local potential client.
How long before I see results from Instagram boosts?You’ll see increased profile visits and new followers within the first week. Real authority-building takes 8–12 weeks of consistent weekly boosts. By month three, local homeowners will recognize your face and start engaging with your organic content too.
