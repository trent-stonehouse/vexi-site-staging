The six tools below are organized by function. Each one solves a different problem. Together, they form a complete AI toolkit for any real estate agent who wants to look professional, produce content fast, and grow without hiring a team.

1. Looka — AI Logo & Brand Identity

🎨

Looka

Branding & Identity

What it does: Looka uses AI to generate professional logos, brand kits, business cards, social media templates, and letterheads — all from a few prompts about your style preferences, colors, and industry.

How agents use it: New agents who need a professional brand identity on day one. Experienced agents rebranding without spending $2,000 on a designer. Team leaders creating sub-brands for team members.

  • Answer a series of style questions (modern vs. classic, icon preferences, colors)
  • AI generates dozens of logo options in under 60 seconds
  • Customize fonts, layout, icon size, and color palette
  • Export a full brand kit: logo files (SVG, PNG, EPS), color codes, font pairings, business card designs, social media profile images

Pricing: Free to design. Basic Logo Package starts at $20 (one-time). Brand Kit subscription starts at $96/year for full brand assets including social media templates and 300+ branded designs.

💡 Agent Tip

Use Looka’s Brand Kit plan instead of the basic logo. It generates pre-sized social media headers, email signatures, and business cards — all matching your brand. That alone saves you 3–4 hours of back-and-forth with a designer.

Traditional logo design costs $500–$2,500 and takes 2–4 weeks. Looka delivers a complete brand kit in under 10 minutes for $20–$96. That is a 95% cost reduction and a 99% time reduction.

2. Remove.bg — Instant Background Removal

✄️

Remove.bg

Photo Editing

What it does: Upload any photo and Remove.bg strips the background in 5 seconds using AI. The result is a clean cutout with transparent background — perfect for headshots, listing graphics, and branded content.

How agents use it:

  • Professional headshots — Remove a messy office background and replace it with a clean solid color or branded backdrop
  • Listing graphics — Isolate a property photo and place it on a custom background with text overlay
  • Social media content — Create “before/after” composites, team photos with consistent backgrounds, and event graphics
  • Yard sign and print designs — Drop your headshot cutout onto any marketing material without Photoshop

Pricing: Free for standard quality (up to 0.25 megapixels). Pay-as-you-go credits start at $0.90 per image for HD quality. Subscription plans from $9/month for 40 credits.

💡 Agent Tip

Combine Remove.bg with Canva. Remove the background from your headshot, drop it into a Canva template, and you have a branded “Just Listed” graphic in under 2 minutes. This is the exact workflow top agents use to produce content fast.

3. Luma AI — 3D Space Capture

🌐

Luma AI

3D & Virtual Tours

What it does: Luma AI turns a video walkthrough from your phone into a photorealistic 3D model of any space. No special camera. No tripod. Just walk through the property recording on your iPhone, upload the video, and Luma’s AI builds a navigable 3D scene.

How agents use it:

  • Listing presentations — Show sellers a 3D model of their home before it hits the market. Instantly differentiates you from every other agent
  • Out-of-town buyers — Let remote buyers explore properties in 3D instead of relying on flat photos
  • Social media content — Share 3D flythrough clips as Reels. These stop the scroll because they look cinematic
  • Open house follow-up — Send a 3D link to every visitor so they can revisit the home digitally

Pricing: Free tier available with limited captures. API access and higher-resolution exports on paid plans. Check lumalabs.ai for current pricing.

💡 Agent Tip

Record your walkthrough slowly and smoothly — steady movement produces dramatically better 3D results. Walk heel-to-toe and keep the camera at chest height. One property takes about 5 minutes to film and 10 minutes for Luma to process.

Listings with 3D virtual tours receive 87% more views than listings with photos only. They also reduce unnecessary in-person showings by filtering out buyers who are not a fit — saving you hours per week.

Source: Realtor.com Market Research, 2025

4. Canva — AI-Powered Design

🎨

Canva

Graphic Design

What it does: Canva is the all-in-one design platform most agents already know — but its 2025–2026 AI updates changed the game. Magic Design generates full layouts from a single prompt. Magic Eraser removes unwanted objects from photos. Magic Write generates copy for any design. And Brand Kit keeps everything on-brand automatically.

How agents use it:

  • Social media graphics — Just Listed, Just Sold, market update carousels, open house flyers
  • Listing presentations — Branded CMA covers, property brochures, buyer guides
  • Video content — Short-form videos with text overlays, transitions, and auto-captions
  • Print materials — Door hangers, postcards, yard sign riders, business cards

Pricing: Free plan covers basic features. Canva Pro at $13/month (billed yearly) unlocks Brand Kit, Magic Design, premium templates, background remover, and 100GB storage. Canva for Teams from $10/person/month.

💡 Agent Tip

Set up your Brand Kit first. Upload your logo, pick your 3 brand colors, and select your fonts. Then every template you open auto-applies your branding. This one setup step eliminates 90% of design decisions going forward.

5. Kapwing — Video Editing Without the Learning Curve

🎥

Kapwing

Video Editing

What it does: Kapwing is a browser-based video editor with AI features that eliminate the hardest parts of video production. Auto-generate captions, resize videos for any platform, remove background noise, trim dead space, and repurpose a single long-form video into multiple short clips — all without downloading software.

How agents use it:

  • Listing walkthroughs — Trim raw footage, add captions, and resize for Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts in one session
  • Market update videos — Record yourself talking, let Kapwing auto-caption and clean up the audio
  • Repurposing — Turn a 10-minute YouTube video into five 60-second Reels using Smart Cut (AI finds the best clips)
  • Client testimonials — Clean up phone-recorded testimonials with noise removal and captions

Pricing: Free plan includes basic editing with watermark. Pro at $16/month (billed yearly) removes watermark, adds AI features, 10GB storage, and priority exports. Business plan at $25/user/month for teams.

💡 Agent Tip

Use Kapwing’s “Repurpose” feature to turn one property walkthrough into 3–5 short-form clips automatically. AI identifies the most engaging moments. One 5-minute video becomes a week of content.

Video posts generate 49% more engagement than image posts on Instagram. Agents who post 2+ videos per week see 3x more DMs than those who only post static graphics. The barrier is not filming — it is editing. AI tools remove that barrier.

Source: Sprout Social, Video Marketing Statistics, 2025

6. Yorbi.ai — AI Virtual Staging

🛋

Yorbi.ai

Virtual Staging

What it does: Yorbi.ai takes photos of empty rooms and fills them with photorealistic, AI-generated furniture and decor. Upload a photo, select a style (modern, farmhouse, minimalist, luxury), and get a staged version in under 30 seconds. The results are good enough for MLS listings.

How agents use it:

  • Vacant listings — Stage every room digitally instead of spending $2,000–$5,000 on physical staging
  • Multiple style options — Show the same room in three different styles to appeal to different buyer personas
  • Listing photos — Upload your photographer’s empty-room shots and deliver staged versions the same day
  • Social media content — Before/after staging comparisons make excellent carousel posts

Pricing: Plans start around $12/month for basic staging credits. Professional plans with unlimited renders and style options are available. Check yorbi.ai for current tiers.

💡 Agent Tip

Always disclose virtual staging in your MLS listings. Include the original empty-room photo alongside the staged version. Buyers appreciate the transparency, and it keeps you compliant with fair housing and MLS rules.

Staged homes sell 73% faster than non-staged homes. Traditional staging costs $2,000–$5,000 per property. AI virtual staging costs under $1 per room. The ROI is not even close.

Source: NAR, Profile of Home Staging, 2025

Build Your AI Toolkit: A 5-Step Action Plan

You do not need all six tools on day one. Here is the order that gives you the fastest ROI, starting with the tools that have the biggest immediate impact.

  1. Start with Canva — It Covers the Most Ground

    Sign up for Canva Pro ($13/month). Set up your Brand Kit with your logo, colors, and fonts. This single tool handles social media graphics, listing presentations, carousels, flyers, and basic video editing. If you only use one tool from this list, make it Canva.

  2. Get Your Brand Right with Looka

    If you do not have a professional logo yet, stop and fix that first. Go to Looka, spend 10 minutes generating options, pick your favorite, and download the brand kit. Upload your new logo into Canva’s Brand Kit. Now everything you create is on-brand from day one.

  3. Add Remove.bg for Instant Photo Polish

    Bookmark Remove.bg and use it every time you need a clean headshot cutout, an isolated property photo, or a transparent PNG for a graphic. The free tier handles most needs. Upgrade only if you are processing more than 10 images per month.

  4. Level Up Video with Kapwing

    Once you are posting consistently (at least 3x/week), add Kapwing to your stack. Use it to auto-caption your videos, repurpose long-form content into Reels, and clean up audio on testimonial clips. Video is the highest-performing content format in 2026 — Kapwing makes it accessible.

  5. Add Luma AI + Yorbi.ai for Listings

    When you have active listings, add these two. Use Luma AI to create 3D walkthroughs from your phone. Use Yorbi.ai to virtually stage vacant rooms. Together, they make every listing look premium — without the premium price tag.

The “Stack” Mentality

Think of these tools as layers in a stack, not replacements for each other. Canva is your foundation. Looka gives you the brand assets Canva needs. Remove.bg polishes your photos for Canva templates. Kapwing handles the video Canva cannot. Luma AI and Yorbi.ai are listing-specific power-ups. Each tool makes the others more effective.

Total investment for the full stack: $50–$150/month. That is less than one client dinner — and it produces content that generates leads every single day.
